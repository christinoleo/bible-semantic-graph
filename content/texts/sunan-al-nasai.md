The abridgment (*Al-Mujtabā*, "the selected") of the larger *Al-Sunan al-Kubrā* compiled by [[ahmad-al-nasai]] (829–915 CE / 215–303 AH). Roughly 5,750 reports, organized by *fiqh* topics. Al-Nasā'ī applied the strictest authentication standards of the four *Sunan* compilers; his *Mujtabā* is consequently the most rigorous of the four.

Stands fourth in the canonical sequence of the *Kutub al-Sittah* after the two *Ṣaḥīḥān* and [[sunan-abu-dawud]] in most Sunni rankings.
