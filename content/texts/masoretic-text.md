The authoritative Hebrew text of the [[hebrew-bible|Hebrew Bible]] in Jewish tradition, transmitted by the *Masoretes* — schools of scribes (Tiberian, Babylonian, Palestinian) active from c. 500 to 1000 CE who fixed the consonantal text, added vocalization (*nikkud*), cantillation marks (*ṭeʿamim*), and the *Masorah* (marginal notes preserving textual minutiae). The Tiberian Ben-Asher tradition became dominant; its earliest complete witnesses are the [[aleppo-codex|Aleppo Codex]] (c. 920 CE, [[aaron-ben-asher|Aaron ben Asher]]) and the [[leningrad-codex|Leningrad Codex]] B19A (1008 CE). Confirmed in extraordinary stability against the [[dead-sea-scrolls|Dead Sea Scrolls]] more than a millennium older.
