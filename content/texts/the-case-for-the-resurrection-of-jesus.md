[[gary-habermas]] and [[michael-licona]]'s 2004 monograph, the standard reference for the [[minimal-facts-approach]] to the [[resurrection-of-jesus]]. The argument rests only on data accepted by the broad consensus of critical NT scholarship — Jesus's death by crucifixion, the disciples' belief that he had appeared to them risen, [[paul]]'s sudden transformation, [[james-brother-of-jesus|James]]'s skeptical conversion, the early date of the [[claim-1-cor-15-creed-earliest-witness|1 Cor 15 creed]] — and argues that the resurrection is the best historical explanation of those data.

Picked up and popularized for a Muslim-Christian audience by [[nabeel-qureshi]] in *[[no-god-but-one]]*.
