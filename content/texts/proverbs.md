Anthology of Israelite wisdom sayings: extended didactic poems on the way of wisdom (Prov 1-9), collections of short proverbial couplets (Prov 10-29), and appended foreign-sage and women-of-substance material (Prov 30-31). Canonical wisdom book sharing genre and vocabulary with ANE sapiential traditions.

Attribution to [[solomon]] in the superscriptions (Prov 1:1; 10:1; 25:1) is the traditional anchor; critical scholarship treats the Solomonic frame as honorific — Solomon as ANE wisdom-patron — while the collections themselves represent generations of scribal compilation spanning the monarchic and post-exilic periods. Prov 22:17-24:22 has long-recognized close parallels (sometimes verbal) with the Egyptian *Instruction of Amenemope* (likely 12th-11th c. BCE), a paradigm case of cross-cultural sapiential borrowing.

Structure: extended discourses on wisdom and folly, including the personification of Lady Wisdom (Prov 8) and the seductress Folly (Prov 1-9); Solomonic proverbs (Prov 10-22:16); "Words of the Wise" (Prov 22:17-24:22); additional Wise sayings (Prov 24:23-34); Hezekian Solomonic collection (Prov 25-29); words of Agur (Prov 30); words of King Lemuel and the acrostic poem on the *eshet hayil* (Prov 31).

Themes: fear of YHWH as the beginning of wisdom (Prov 1:7), the two ways (wise and foolish), retributive optimism — wisdom delivers, folly destroys — held in canonical tension with the protests of [[book-of-job]] and [[ecclesiastes]]; sexual ethics, speech ethics, economic ethics, royal counsel; Wisdom as cosmic principle (Prov 8:22-31, taken up in later Jewish *Sophia* theology and in Christian Logos Christology).

Reception: foundational for rabbinic ethics (*Pirkei Avot* picks up the genre) and patristic catechesis. Prov 8 is among the most exegetically contested passages in the Hebrew Bible — the Arian controversy of the fourth century turned on the verb in Prov 8:22 (LXX *ektisen*, "created"), with massive christological stakes. Modern reception emphasizes Proverbs' ANE context and its complicated wisdom-and-gender politics.
