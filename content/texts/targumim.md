Aramaic translations and paraphrases of the [[hebrew-bible|Hebrew Bible]] produced over centuries in Babylonia and Palestine for liturgical and scholarly use as Aramaic replaced Hebrew as the spoken language of Jewish communities. The principal corpora are *Targum Onkelos* (formally precise, on the Pentateuch), *Targum Jonathan ben Uzziel* (on the Prophets), and the more expansive *Targum Pseudo-Jonathan* and the *Fragment Targums*. Important witnesses to Second-Temple and rabbinic interpretation and to the Aramaic substrate of the Gospel sayings of [[jesus]].
