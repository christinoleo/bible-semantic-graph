The first of the four *Sunan* collections in the *Kutub al-Sittah*, compiled by [[abu-dawud-al-sijistani]] (817–889 CE / 202–275 AH). Roughly 4,800 reports drawn from an estimated 500,000, focused tightly on *aḥādīth al-aḥkām* — reports bearing on legal rulings (*fiqh*).

Abū Dāwūd's stated method is to record the strongest reports on each legal point and to flag weak ones explicitly with his own commentary. The collection became the working hadith reference for early Sunni jurists and remains one of the six canonical books.
