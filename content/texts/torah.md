The first five books of the [[hebrew-bible|Hebrew Bible]] — [[genesis|Genesis]], [[exodus|Exodus]], [[leviticus|Leviticus]], [[numbers|Numbers]], [[deuteronomy|Deuteronomy]] — traditionally ascribed to [[moses|Moses]] and forming the foundational legal-narrative corpus of Judaism. In Christianity it constitutes the opening section of the Old Testament; in Islam, the *Tawrāt* is identified with the original revelation given to Moses ([[quran|Quran]] 5:44, 3:3, 5:46). Modern critical scholarship via the [[documentary-hypothesis]] proposes a composite redactional origin (J, E, D, P sources).
