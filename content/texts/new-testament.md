The Christian scriptures, comprising the four canonical Gospels ([[gospel-of-matthew|Matthew]], [[gospel-of-mark|Mark]], [[gospel-of-luke|Luke]], [[gospel-of-john|John]]), [[acts|Acts of the Apostles]], the [[pauline-epistles|Pauline epistles]] (thirteen letters), the General Epistles ([[hebrews|Hebrews]], [[james|James]], 1-2 [[1-peter|Peter]], 1-3 [[1-john|John]], [[jude|Jude]]), and [[revelation|Revelation]] — twenty-seven books in the closed canon. Composed in Koine Greek between c. 50 and 110 CE — entirely within the lifetime of [[jesus]]'s eyewitnesses or those who heard from them. The definitive Christian testimony to Jesus's life, death, resurrection, and the apostolic founding of the Church.
