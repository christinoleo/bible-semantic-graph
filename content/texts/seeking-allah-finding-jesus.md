[[nabeel-qureshi]]'s 2014 conversion memoir, structured as a multi-year investigation triggered by debates with his college friend [[david-wood]] at Old Dominion University. The narrative arc moves from his [[ahmadiyya]] upbringing through three investigative phases: (i) the [[trinity]] and the deity of [[jesus]] (Qureshi's first objection); (ii) the historicity of the [[crucifixion-of-jesus]] and [[resurrection-of-jesus]] against the Q 4:157 / [[claim-ahmadiyya-survival-thesis|Kashmir-survival]] reading; (iii) the comparative case for the [[bible]] versus the [[quran]] (textual transmission, [[tahrif]]).

Less systematic than *[[no-god-but-one]]* — its argumentative core is biographical: the *change-of-mind* itself is the artifact being explained. Functions as the pastoral / experiential complement to the later book.
