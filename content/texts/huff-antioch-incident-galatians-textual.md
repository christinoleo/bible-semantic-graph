[[wesley-huff]]'s textual-critical examination of the variant in Galatians 2:12 — whether [[paul]] originally wrote ἦλθον (plural, "*they* came from James") or ἦλθεν (singular, "*he* came from James"). The variant decides who exactly arrived at Antioch and prompted [[peter]]'s separation from the Gentile believers — a single envoy from [[james-brother-of-jesus|James]], or a delegation. The paper argues from manuscript witnesses, scribal-tendency analysis, and internal Pauline rhetoric for a specific reading; the conclusion has implications for reconstructing the Jerusalem-Antioch dynamic in the apostolic period.
