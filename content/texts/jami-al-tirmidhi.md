Compiled by [[muhammad-al-tirmidhi]] (824–892 CE / 209–279 AH), a student of [[muhammad-al-bukhari]] and one of his most influential successors. Roughly 3,950 reports organized by legal-topical books, distinguished from the other *Sunan* by its juristic commentary: al-Tirmidhī typically records the report, then surveys the legal positions of the major *madhāhib* (Sunni schools of law) on the question.

He introduces the explicit grading category *ḥasan* (good — between ṣaḥīḥ and ḍaʿīf) and applies it systematically, an analytical move that becomes standard in later hadith criticism. The work is therefore as much a comparative juristic compendium as a hadith collection.
