[[nabeel-qureshi]]'s 2016 systematic apologetic, structured as eight comparison questions between Islam and Christianity: (1) same God? (2) [[trinity]] vs. [[tawhid]]; (3) deity of [[jesus]] vs. prophethood-only; (4) did Jesus die on the cross? (5) the [[resurrection-of-jesus]]; (6) is [[muhammad]] a prophet of God? (7) is the [[quran]] the word of God? (8) salvation by grace vs. by works. Each pair is argued on shared evidential ground rather than from confessional prooftexts.

The historical-resurrection section is a popularization of the [[minimal-facts-approach]] he learned from his doctoral supervisor [[gary-habermas]] and Habermas's co-author [[michael-licona]]. The Quran-vs-Bible section foregrounds the [[islamic-dilemma]] — the structural tension between Q 5:46-47 commanding Christians to judge by their Gospel and the later [[tahrif]] tradition explaining the Gospel's divergence from the Quran by corruption.
