The victory poem in [[judges|Judges]] 5 celebrating the defeat of Sisera by an Israelite tribal coalition under [[deborah]] and [[barak|Barak]] — and especially [[jael|Jael]]'s killing of Sisera with a tent-peg. Widely regarded by historical-critical scholarship as one of the oldest preserved Hebrew texts (twelfth or eleventh century BCE on linguistic grounds — pre-monarchic), making it a primary source for early Israelite tribal religion, military organization, and pre-Davidic theology.
