The sixth and last-admitted book of the *Kutub al-Sittah*, compiled by [[ibn-majah]] (824–887 CE / 209–273 AH). Roughly 4,340 reports organized by legal-topical books, of which a meaningful minority (some estimates ~30% of unique reports) are graded below *ṣaḥīḥ* by later critics — a weaker authentication profile than the other five canonical collections.

Inclusion in the canonical six was a gradual reception decision, formalized in the late medieval period and not universally accepted; some Maghrebi and Andalusi traditions substituted Mālik's *Muwaṭṭaʾ* in the sixth slot.
