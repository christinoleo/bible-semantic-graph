[[wesley-huff]]'s academic paper on the textual evidence for high [[claim-high-christology-in-earliest-strata|Christology]] in the [[gospel-of-john]] — examining whether the explicit deity-claims in John (the "I am" sayings, John 1:1, John 8:58, John 10:30) are textually stable across the manuscript tradition or whether they appear to be later editorial additions. Argues against the popular-skeptical reading (Ehrman-tradition) that John's high Christology is a late accretion; the textual variants surrounding the key Christological pericopes are minor and do not destabilize the explicit divine claims.
