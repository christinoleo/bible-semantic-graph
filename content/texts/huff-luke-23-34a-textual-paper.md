[[wesley-huff]]'s textual-critical paper on the disputed status of Luke 23:34a — "Father, forgive them, for they do not know what they do" — one of the most famous "missing verses" in NT textual criticism, omitted by major early witnesses (𝔓⁷⁵, Sinaiticus first hand, Vaticanus, Bezae corrected, others) and present in the Majority Text and most modern translations. The paper assesses the manuscript distribution, the scribal tendencies that might explain omission or insertion, and the internal-Lukan stylistic evidence for authenticity. The case study illustrates [[huff]]'s general program: that high-profile textual variants, fairly examined, do not destabilize Christian doctrine — see [[claim-textual-variants-do-not-affect-essential-doctrine]].
