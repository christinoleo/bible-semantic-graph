The Jewish scriptures, comprising the [[torah|Torah]] (Five Books of Moses), *Nevi'im* (Prophets — Joshua through Malachi), and *Ketuvim* (Writings — Psalms, Proverbs, Job, the Five Megillot, Daniel, Ezra-Nehemiah, Chronicles). The acronym *TaNaKh* spells the three sections. Christian Old Testament canons coincide in content for Protestants, expand to include deuterocanonical books for Catholics and Orthodox, and arrange the same books in a different order (ending in prophecy rather than Chronicles). The Quran refers repeatedly to this corpus and its prophets — sometimes by name (*Tawrāt*) and sometimes thematically — as authentic prior revelation.
