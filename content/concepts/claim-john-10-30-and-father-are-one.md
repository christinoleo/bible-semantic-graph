[[jesus]] declares in John 10:30, "*Egō kai ho patēr hen esmen* — I and the Father are one"; his hearers immediately pick up stones, charging blasphemy "because you, being a man, make yourself God" (John 10:33). The narrative thus reads the saying — and Jesus accepts it being read — as a claim to divine identity.
