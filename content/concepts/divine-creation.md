The doctrine that the cosmos and everything in it was brought into being by God — classically in [[genesis|Genesis]] 1:1 ("In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth") and developed in subsequent Jewish, Christian, and Islamic theology as *creatio ex nihilo*: creation out of nothing, not out of preexisting matter. Distinguishes monotheistic creation from emanationist (Plotinian) and demiurgic (Platonic, Gnostic) cosmologies. Provides the conclusion of the [[kalam-cosmological-argument|Kalām Cosmological Argument]] and the foundational claim contested by atheist and pantheist cosmologies.
