The view that "only what science can establish counts as knowledge" — scientism — is itself not a scientific claim but a philosophical one; if true, it would be unknowable by its own criterion. As [[john-lennox|Lennox]] presses against the New Atheists ([[richard-dawkins|Dawkins]], Hitchens), scientism is *self-defeating*: the proposition is metaphysics masquerading as physics. Science is a tremendously powerful method within its domain (empirically testable claims about physical regularities) but cannot adjudicate questions about meaning, ethics, mathematics, or its own epistemic warrant.
