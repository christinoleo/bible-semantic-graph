The position that the [[second-coming-of-christ|return of Christ]] occurs *before* the thousand-year reign of [[revelation|Revelation]] 20:4-6 — Christ returns, defeats the [[antichrist|Beast]], *and then* inaugurates the millennium on a renewed earth. Held by early patristic writers ([[justin-martyr|Justin Martyr]], [[irenaeus|Irenaeus]], [[tertullian|Tertullian]]) and by both [[dispensationalism|dispensational premillennialists]] and "historic premillennialists" (George Eldon Ladd).
