The mainstream Sunni response to the [[ahmadiyya|Ahmadi]] movement — that its founder's prophetic and messianic claims violate [[khatam-an-nabiyyin|the seal of the prophets]] at Q 33:40 and therefore constitute *kufr* — has been formalized juridically (Pakistan's Second Amendment, 1974, defines Ahmadis as non-Muslim; Ordinance XX, 1984, criminalizes Ahmadi religious self-description) and theologically (Al-Azhar fatāwā). The exclusion is the historical and political context for [[nabeel-qureshi]]'s family migration to the United States and his minority-within-a-minority identity in *[[seeking-allah-finding-jesus]]*.
