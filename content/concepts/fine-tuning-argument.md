The argument that the values of the fundamental physical constants and the initial conditions of the universe lie within an extraordinarily narrow range compatible with life, and that this specified improbability is better explained by a designer than by chance or physical necessity. Multiverse responses face the question of whether the multiverse-generating mechanism is itself fine-tuned. The argument is widely regarded as the strongest contemporary form of the [[teleological-argument|design argument]].
