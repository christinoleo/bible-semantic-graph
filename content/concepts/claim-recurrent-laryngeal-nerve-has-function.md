The recurrent laryngeal nerve's detour around the aortic arch, frequently cited as [[claim-suboptimal-biology-refutes-design|evidence of suboptimal biology]], in fact serves integrated developmental and physiological roles — connecting heart and larynx as the embryo elongates and providing collateral innervation to the esophagus, trachea, and pericardium — making it a feature of [[teleological-argument|coordinated design]] rather than wasteful routing. This reframes the [[argument-from-poor-design|alleged dysteleology]] as a constraint-respecting solution consistent with the [[existence-of-god|theistic-design hypothesis]].
