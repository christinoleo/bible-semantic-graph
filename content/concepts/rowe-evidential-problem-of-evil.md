[[william-rowe]] (1979): even granting that the *logical* incompatibility argument fails, the existence of apparently *gratuitous* evil — paradigmatically a fawn slowly dying in a forest fire no observer could redeem — gives strong inductive evidence against an omnipotent, omnibenevolent God. The argument is probabilistic: such suffering is more likely under naturalism than under [[classical-theism]].
