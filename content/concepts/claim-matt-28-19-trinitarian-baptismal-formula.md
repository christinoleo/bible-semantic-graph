The risen [[jesus]] commands the eleven to baptize "*eis to onoma tou patros kai tou huiou kai tou hagiou pneumatos* — into the name (singular) of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit." That the three are joined under one *onoma* (not three names) and ranked together as the *terminus* of baptismal allegiance is read by patristic exegesis as the dominical formulation of the [[trinity]].
