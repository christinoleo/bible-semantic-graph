Patristic and Reformed exegesis read [[jesus]]'s reply to the rich young ruler as a Socratic counter-question: he tests the ruler's category, asking whether the ruler is willing to draw the inference his own address implies — that calling Jesus "good" entails calling him divine. The saying is a rhetorical pivot inviting recognition, not Jesus disowning the predicate.
