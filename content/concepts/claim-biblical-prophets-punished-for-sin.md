When biblical prophets sin, God severely reprimands and punishes them — [[david|David]] is rebuked by Nathan and his child dies (2 Sam 12); [[moses|Moses]] is barred from entering the Promised Land for striking the rock (Num 20:12); [[abraham|Abraham]] is rebuked over Sarah at Pharaoh's court and again with Abimelech. Crucially, the prophets never claim God gave them *permission to bypass* moral law. By contrast, [[muhammad]]'s contested actions are accompanied by [[claim-muhammad-self-exempting-revelations-surah-33|specific revelations granting him exemptions]] — a structurally different pattern.
