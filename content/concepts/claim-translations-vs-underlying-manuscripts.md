The KJV, NIV, and ESV are distinct English translations of the *same* underlying Hebrew (Old Testament), Aramaic (portions of Daniel/Ezra), and Greek (New Testament) manuscripts — the [[masoretic-text]], the Septuagint where relevant, and the Greek New Testament critical editions. Surface-level wording differences arise from translation philosophy (formal vs dynamic equivalence) and target English register, not from textual disagreement. Conflating translation diversity with manuscript corruption is a category error.
