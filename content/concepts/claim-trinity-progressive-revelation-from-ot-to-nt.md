Christian Trinitarian apologetics holds that the doctrine is not a post-biblical invention but the dogmatic articulation of patterns present across the canonical scriptures: the plural divine consultation in [[genesis|Gen]] 1:26 ("let *us* make man in *our* image"); the threefold Trisagion in Isa 6:3; Wisdom personified in Prov 8 (the basis of [[claim-prov-8-22-31-wisdom-personification-christian-reading|patristic Wisdom Christology]]); the Spirit hovering over the waters in Gen 1:2; and in the [[new-testament|NT]] the explicit triadic formulas of [[claim-matt-28-19-trinitarian-baptismal-formula|Mt 28:19]], 2 Cor 13:14, 1 Pet 1:2, and the [[claim-mark-1-10-11-trinitarian-theophany-at-baptism|baptismal theophanies]]. The doctrine emerges as the *grammar of the data*, not as imposition on it.
