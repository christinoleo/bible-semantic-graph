The historical record in *[[sahih-al-bukhari|Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī]]* (6130, 5158) and *[[sunan-al-nasai|Sunan an-Nasāʾī]]* (3378) reports that [[muhammad]] married [[aisha|ʿĀʾisha]] when she was six and consummated the marriage when she was nine, while she still played with dolls. Christian apologetics treats this as a foundational moral problem incompatible with Muhammad's status as moral exemplar; Islamic apologetics responds either through cultural-relativist appeal ("times were different"), through arguments that ʿĀʾisha had reached puberty, or through alternate dating proposals (e.g., a much older ʿĀʾisha — a minority revisionist position).
