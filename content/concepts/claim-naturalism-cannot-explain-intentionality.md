[[intentionality|Intentionality]] — the "aboutness" by which thoughts represent things beyond themselves — resists reduction to the purely physical, since any proposed [[naturalism|naturalistic]] base (causal, functional, biological) either already presupposes semantic content or fails to fix determinate content, as [[john-searle]], [[jerry-fodor]], and [[edward-feser]] each argue from different angles. On [[theism]], where [[mind]] is metaphysically prior to matter, [[intentionality]] is a basic feature of reality rather than an unexplained emergent anomaly, so the [[philosophy-of-mind|mind–world]] gap that [[naturalism]] cannot close is dissolved.
