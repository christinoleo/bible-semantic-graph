[[alvin-plantinga|Plantinga]]'s reformulation in modal logic (S5): (P1) It is *possible* that a maximally great being exists (a being that is omnipotent, omniscient, morally perfect *in every possible world*). (P2) If maximal greatness is possible, then a maximally great being exists in some possible world. (P3) If such a being exists in some possible world, it exists in every possible world (because maximal greatness includes necessary existence). (P4) Therefore it exists in the actual world. The argument's controversial premise is P1 — whether the very *possibility* of maximal greatness can be granted to the theist by the atheist.
