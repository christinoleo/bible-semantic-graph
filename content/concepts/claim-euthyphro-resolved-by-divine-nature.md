The classical Euthyphro dilemma — is the good loved by God because it is good, or good because loved by God — assumes a false dichotomy. The modified divine command theory (Robert Adams, William Alston, Craig) grounds moral *values* in God's necessary, perfectly good nature, and moral *duties* in God's commands which flow from that nature. Morality is therefore neither arbitrary (God could not command cruelty because his nature prohibits it) nor independent of God (the standard *is* God's nature). The third horn dissolves the dilemma.
