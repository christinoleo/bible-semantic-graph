The Servant Songs identify the Servant explicitly with [[israel]] (Isa 41:8-9, 44:1-2, 44:21, 45:4, 49:3 — "You are my servant, Israel, in whom I will be glorified"). Collective-corporate suffering of exile-and-restoration is the unified frame of Deutero-Isaiah, not messianic individual prophecy of [[jesus]].
