Sean Carroll argues that the well-confirmed "Core Theory" of physics (Standard Model + General Relativity) is empirically complete for the everyday domain (energies below ~1 TeV) — meaning all phenomena humans experience are described in principle by known physics, with no causal gap requiring divine intervention. Combined with biology (Darwinian evolution), neuroscience (mind from brain), and cosmology (a self-contained universe via quantum cosmology), the naturalist worldview is *explanatorily complete* in a way that makes God explanatorily idle. By Ockham's Razor, the parsimonious hypothesis prevails. Christian counter: this argument *presupposes* naturalism in its delimiting of "everyday domain" and ignores the philosophical questions (existence of laws themselves, fine-tuning, consciousness, intentionality) where naturalism is precisely *not* explanatorily complete.
