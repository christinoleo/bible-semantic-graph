The cognitive readiness for theistic belief that [[cognitive-science-of-religion|CSR]] documents is *exactly* what the Reformed doctrine of the *sensus divinitatis* — articulated by [[john-calvin|Calvin]] and formalized epistemically by [[alvin-plantinga|Plantinga]] in *Warranted Christian Belief* — predicts: God designed human cognition to form belief in Him naturally and properly-basically under appropriate triggering conditions. Read through [[reformed-epistemology|Reformed epistemology]], the [[claim-csr-natural-origin-of-religion|CSR data]] is evidence *for* the [[existence-of-god|theistic hypothesis]], since a designed faculty would be expected to operate via ordinary cognitive mechanisms like [[theory-of-mind|theory-of-mind]] and [[agency-detection|agency-detection]] rather than against them.
