The Servant Songs across [[book-of-isaiah]] individuate progressively — corporate [[israel]] (Isa 42:1), then a righteous-Israel-within-Israel whose mission is *to* Israel (49:3-5), then a single suffering individual *distinct from* the nation he atones for (52:13-53:12). The narrowing climaxes in messianic-individual reference that [[jesus]] fulfills, since the Servant of Isaiah 53 cannot be identical with the people he restores.
