Justin Barrett, a Christian developmental psychologist and major [[cognitive-science-of-religion|CSR]] researcher, argues that CSR describes the *mechanism* by which humans form religious beliefs but is strictly silent on the *truth* of those beliefs — the [[claim-csr-natural-origin-of-religion|debunking inference]] smuggles in a naturalistic premise the data does not supply. Mechanisms like the [[hyperactive-agency-detection|hyperactive agency-detection device (HADD)]] and [[theory-of-mind|theory-of-mind]] could equally be malfunctioning false-positive generators *or* truth-tracking faculties designed by God to detect Him, and the empirical findings of *Born Believers* (2012) — that children are cognitive natives to theism — are at least as comfortable on the second reading as the first.
