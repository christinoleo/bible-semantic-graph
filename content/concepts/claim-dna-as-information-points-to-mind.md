The DNA molecule encodes roughly 3.5 billion letters of functionally-specified information whose every known parallel — language, software, blueprints — traces to a mind. As [[john-lennox|Lennox]] argues, biology *uses linguistic vocabulary* to describe DNA (transcription, translation, code, message) not metaphorically but because the molecule *is* a semiotic system; the inference from specified information to a designing intelligence is the standard inference everywhere else and should be granted here too.
