[[daniel-9]]'s Seventy Weeks (Dan 9:24-27) pinpoint the [[messiah]]'s death: counting 483 prophetic years (sixty-nine weeks of years) from Artaxerxes's decree to rebuild Jerusalem (445 BCE) terminates near 30 CE, matching [[jesus]]'s crucifixion. "An anointed one shall be cut off and shall have nothing" (Dan 9:26) is fulfilled in Jesus, with the destruction of the Second Temple (70 CE) confirming the prophetic frame.
