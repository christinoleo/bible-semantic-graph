The Islamic apologetic argument that [[aisha|ʿĀʾisha]] had reached menarche / physical maturity at the time the marriage was consummated, drawing on alternate readings of the hadith chronology or on regional-climate claims about earlier puberty onset. The argument is directly contradicted by *[[sahih-al-bukhari|Bukhārī]]* 6130, which states ʿĀʾisha played with dolls *because* she had not yet reached the age of puberty.
