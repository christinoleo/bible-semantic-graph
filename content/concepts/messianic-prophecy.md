Category of Hebrew Bible passages read across [[christianity]] and [[judaism]] as foretelling a future anointed deliverer ([[messiah]]). [[christian-apologetic]] reading treats the corpus cumulatively — Isa 7:14, 9:6-7, 11:1-10, 53; Mic 5:2; Zech 9:9, 12:10; Dan 9:24-27; Ps 22, 110 and others — as converging on [[jesus]] of Nazareth, with the convergence itself (not any single text in isolation) doing the apologetic work. [[jewish-anti-missionary]] exegesis disputes both the individual referents (claiming national-Israel, Hezekiah, Cyrus, or generic-righteous readings) and the legitimacy of the cumulative move. The disagreement is therefore both verse-by-verse and methodological.
