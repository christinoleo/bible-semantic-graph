[[anselm-of-canterbury|Anselm]]'s pivotal premise in *Proslogion* 2: a being that exists *in re* (in reality) is greater than a being that exists only *in intellectu* (in the understanding). Therefore "that than which nothing greater can be conceived" — if it exists only in the understanding — would not be the greatest conceivable being, because a greater (one existing also in reality) could be conceived. The premise is challenged by [[claim-kant-existence-not-a-predicate|Kant]]'s argument that existence is not a predicate that contributes to a thing's greatness.
