Well-known textual variants in the NT manuscripts — the longer ending of Mark (16:9-20), the *pericope adulterae* (John 7:53-8:11), the Comma Johanneum, and thousands of minor variants — indicate broader instability of the Christian textual tradition. The pattern supports the general thesis of [[tahrif]].
