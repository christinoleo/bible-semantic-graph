Modern medical analysis of Roman crucifixion — scourging trauma, asphyxiation mechanics, the spear-thrust in [[gospel-of-john]] 19:34 — makes survival of [[jesus]] in any condition to walk to Kashmir physically implausible. The swoon thesis underwriting Ahmadiyya and similar survival readings does not survive contact with the documented lethality of the Roman practice.
