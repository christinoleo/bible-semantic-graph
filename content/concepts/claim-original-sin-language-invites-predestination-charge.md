The parallel Muslim move to the [[claim-fully-god-language-invites-partialism-charge|"fully God" twist]]: when the Christian says humans are "born in sin," the Muslim interlocutor re-routes that into a charge of [[predestination|divine predestination of sin]] — that the Christian God creates people pre-condemned — to force the Christian onto theodicy ground where [[islam]]'s [[fitra]] doctrine (born innocent, accountable only for personal acts) appears morally cleaner. Christian apologists answering [[muhammad-hijab]]-style opponents are advised to phrase [[original-sin]] in language that pre-empts this re-routing.
