[[john-lennox|Lennox]]'s reply to Stephen Hawking's claim (*The Grand Design*, 2010) that "the universe can and will create itself from nothing because of the law of gravity": the laws of physics *describe* regularities, they do not *generate* anything. Newton's laws of motion never moved a billiard ball; they describe what moves. To say the law of gravity created the universe is a category error confusing description with causal agency — and asks the reader to choose between "God or physics" when those are not rival categories at all.
