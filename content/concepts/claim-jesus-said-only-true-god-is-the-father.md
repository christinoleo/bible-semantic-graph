In the high-priestly prayer, [[jesus]] addresses the Father as "*ton monon alēthinon theon* — the only true God" (John 17:3), distinguishing himself from this God as the one whom the Father has sent. The Islamic reading takes this as Jesus's own affirmation that the Father — and not himself — is the singular true deity.
