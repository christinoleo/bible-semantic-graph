An actually infinite collection of concrete things — including past events — cannot exist, because such collections generate contradictions (Hilbert's Hotel, Tristram-Shandy, the infinite library). If the past were actually infinite the present moment could never be reached by successive addition; therefore the past is finite, supporting [[claim-universe-began-to-exist]] and ultimately the [[kalam-cosmological-argument]].
