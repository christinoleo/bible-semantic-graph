[[gospel-of-john]] 15:26 and 16:13 identify the Paraclete as "the Spirit of truth" (τὸ πνεῦμα τῆς ἀληθείας). The explicit pneumatological label fixes the referent as the Holy Spirit, not a future human prophet.
