[[pauline-epistles|1 Corinthians]] 7:36 — written c. 53–54 CE, roughly 600 years before [[muhammad|Muhammad]] — uses the Greek *hyperakmos* ("past the flower / past the prime") to specify that a woman should be physically mature before marriage. The verse establishes a Christian normative standard against child marriage centuries prior to Islam, undercutting the "times were different" defense: the relevant comparative standard was already in place in the very tradition Islamic apologetics treats as foundational scripture.
