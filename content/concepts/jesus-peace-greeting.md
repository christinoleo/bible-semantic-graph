[[jesus]] greets his disciples with *εἰρήνη ὑμῖν* (Luke 24:36, John 20:19, 20:21, 20:26) — the Greek for the underlying Aramaic *šlāmā ʿalkhōn* / Hebrew *šālōm ʿalēkhem*, a Second Temple Jewish formula attested in the Hebrew Bible (Judg 6:23; 1 Sam 25:6; Ps 122:6-8) and rabbinic tradition. The shared Semitic root š-l-m / س-ل-م underlies the Arabic *as-salāmu ʿalaykum*, used in both Jewish-Arabic and later Islamic practice. Whether this linguistic continuity carries religious-theological continuity is contested — see the `has_case` and `has_refuting_case` Nodes for the partisan readings.
