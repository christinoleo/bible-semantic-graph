The first premise of [[craig-moral-argument|Craig's moral argument]]: without a transcendent moral lawgiver, objective moral *values* (it really is wrong to torture innocents for fun) and objective moral *duties* (we really ought not to) have no adequate ontological grounding. Secular alternatives — moral Platonism (abstract objects), constructivism (rational agreement), evolutionary moral realism (Wielenberg's "godless normative facts") — either reduce morality to non-moral facts (and thereby fail to bind) or posit free-floating moral facts whose connection to physical reality is mysterious.
