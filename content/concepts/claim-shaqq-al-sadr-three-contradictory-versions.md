The chest-splitting narrative appears in three substantially different forms in the canonical sources: a childhood version in *[[sahih-muslim|Muslim]]* 162 (among Banū Saʿd, witnessed by [[muhammad|Muhammad]]'s foster-brother), an adolescent or pre-*Mabʿath* version in some sirah parallels, and a *Miʿrāj* version in *[[sahih-al-bukhari|Bukhārī]]* 3207 (in Mecca on the night of the heavenly ascent). Each is presented as the *original* purification. Either the prophet's heart was opened and cleansed multiple times — undermining the doctrine of *ʿiṣma* each time — or the hadith corpus has unstable transmission on a foundational miraculous event. Sunni hadith critics across history (cited e.g. by al-Suhaylī, Ibn Hajar) registered exactly this difficulty.
