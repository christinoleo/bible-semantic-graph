The conciliatory Muslim move when pressed on the Christian title *Son of God*: distinguish *honorific* sonship (calling someone "son" as a term of endearment or respect, as the Arab uncle calls the younger man *ibnī*) from *begotten* sonship (a literal-metaphysical filial relation). On this distinction, Muslims can accept the Bible's "Son of God" language for [[jesus]] in the honorific register while still rejecting the [[trinity|Trinitarian]]–[[chalcedonian-christology|Chalcedonian]] reading as [[shirk]]. The move is closed off by [[claim-q19-93-closes-honorific-son-reading|Q 19:93]], which the Quran itself uses to bar even the metaphorical relation.
