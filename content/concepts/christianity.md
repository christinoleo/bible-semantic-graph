Religion centered on the life, death, and resurrection of [[jesus]] of Nazareth, with sacred scripture comprising the Hebrew Bible (received as the Old Testament) and the New Testament. Spans multiple branches — [[catholic]], [[eastern-orthodox]], [[reformed]], evangelical, and others — each carrying its own creedal, liturgical, and exegetical inheritance and each treated as a distinct Tradition where relevant. Shares scriptural and prophetic roots with [[judaism]] and stands in long apologetic dialogue with [[islam]]. This Node is the religion frame; the corresponding apologetic tradition is [[christian-apologetic]].
