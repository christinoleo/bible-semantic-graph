The project of arguing for God's existence and attributes using reason and observation of nature alone — without appeal to special revelation (scripture, prophecy, religious experience). Classically developed in [[thomas-aquinas|Aquinas]]'s *Summa Theologiae* and revived in analytic philosophy of religion by figures like [[alvin-plantinga|Plantinga]], [[william-lane-craig|Craig]], and Richard Swinburne. The standard repertoire is the cluster of arguments under [[existence-of-god]].
