[[1-john|1 John]] 4:3 — *"every spirit that does not confess Jesus is not from God; this is the spirit of the antichrist"* — provides the diagnostic criterion. The Islamic [[returning-isa-islamic-eschatology|returning ʿĪsā]] explicitly denies the crucifixion, denies divine sonship, and — per *[[sahih-al-bukhari|Bukhārī]]* 2222 — descends precisely *to abolish the cross, kill the pig, and end the jizya* (forcibly ending Christianity). A "Jesus" who returns to destroy the work of Jesus is, by the Johannine definition, the spirit of antichrist itself — not the [[second-coming-of-christ|Second Coming]] but its counterfeit (Mt 24:24).
