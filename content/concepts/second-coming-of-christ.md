The Christian doctrine that [[jesus]] will return personally, visibly, and bodily to judge the living and the dead, defeat the [[antichrist]], establish his kingdom, and consummate the new creation — taught in Mt 24-25, Acts 1:11, 1 Cor 15, 1 Thess 4:13-17, 2 Thess 2, Rev 19:11-21. Crucially, the returning Christ confirms — not denies — the crucifixion, the resurrection, and his own deity (Rev 1:8, 22:13), arrives as Judge and conqueror riding the [[white-horse-rider-of-revelation|white horse of Rev 19]], and is worshiped as God. The contrast with the [[returning-isa-islamic-eschatology|Islamic returning ʿĪsā]] — who denies the cross and the deity — is total.
