The Quranic category for Jews and Christians (and, in some classical readings, Sabians and Zoroastrians) — communities that previously received divine scripture: the [[torah]] given to [[moses]] and the [[injil]] given to [[jesus]]. The status carries practical Islamic-legal consequences: their food is lawful for Muslims, Muslim men may marry their women (Q 5:5), and they pay *jizya* rather than face conversion-or-sword under classical *dhimma* arrangements (Q 9:29). Theologically, the category sustains the [[quranic-confirmation-doctrine]] framing — Ahl al-Kitāb already hold a portion of the same divine message — while opening the polemical surface for the [[tahrif]] charge that the texts they hold have been altered. Muslim interlocutors regularly invoke the category in interfaith dialogue to mark Christians and Jews as honoured rather than condemned, even where they reject the Quran.
