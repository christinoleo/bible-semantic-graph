[[augustine-of-hippo]]: evil is *privatio boni* — the privation of due good, not a positive substance God created — and suffering enters creation through the free fall of rational creatures, first angelic then human. God ordains the resulting evil within providence as instrumental toward greater goods such as redemption and the manifestation of justice.
