The priestly lineage descending from Zadok, [[david|David]]'s high priest (2 Sam 15:24-29) and Solomon's anointing priest (1 Kgs 1:38-39), which held the [[temple|Jerusalem Temple]] high-priesthood through the First-Temple period and into the Second-Temple period until displaced by the Hasmoneans (c. 152 BCE). [[ezekiel|Ezekiel]] 40-48 reserves the eschatological priesthood specifically to the *B'nei Tzadok*. The [[qumran|Qumran]] community self-identifies as the legitimate Zadokite remnant in protest at the Hasmonean and Herodian high-priestly succession; the Sadducees of the Gospels are also etymologically Zadokite.
