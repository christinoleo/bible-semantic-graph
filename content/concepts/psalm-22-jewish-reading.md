[[psalm-22]] is [[david]]'s lament — the superscription assigns it to David, and the [[masoretic-text]] reads כָּאֲרִי ("like a lion at my hands and feet"), not the Christian-preferred כָּארוּ ("they pierced"). The [[septuagint]]/NT divergence reflects a translator's choice (and possibly a different *Vorlage*), not predictive prophecy of crucifixion.
