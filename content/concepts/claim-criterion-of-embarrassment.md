A nascent Jewish messianic movement would not invent a humiliating Roman crucifixion for its messiah, since Deut 21:23 frames "anyone hung on a tree" as cursed and [[paul]] himself calls the cross a "stumbling block" (1 Cor 1:23). Inventions don't take this shape — the crucifixion is too embarrassing to be a literary fiction.
