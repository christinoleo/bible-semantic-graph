Any multiverse-generating mechanism (eternal inflation, string-landscape, ekpyrotic bounce) is itself a precisely-structured physical process with its own free parameters — the inflaton potential, the metric of the landscape, the bounce dynamics — that must themselves take particular values for a universe-generating multiverse to result. The fine-tuning problem is not eliminated but pushed back one level, where it reappears as the fine-tuning of the multiverse-generating mechanism. Defended by Robin Collins; the move is the principal Christian response to the multiverse objection.
