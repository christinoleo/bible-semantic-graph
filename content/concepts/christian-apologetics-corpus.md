The meta-hub for Christian apologetic engagements organized in this graph: classical natural theology arguing for [[existence-of-god|the existence of God]] (the five modern arguments), comparative-religion engagements with Islam ([[bible-vs-quran-preservation|Bible vs Quran preservation]], [[jesus-was-a-muslim-prophet|the Islamic Jesus claim]], [[moral-character-of-muhammad|the moral character of Muhammad]], [[islamic-dilemma|the Islamic Dilemma]], [[eschatology-islam-christianity-parallel|comparative eschatology]]), historicity defenses (the [[crucifixion-of-jesus|crucifixion]] and [[jesus-self-testimony|Jesus's self-testimony]]), and doctrinal defenses (the [[trinity|Trinity]] against [[tawhid|tawḥīd]]; the resurrection against the [[claim-ahmadiyya-survival-thesis|survival thesis]]). Each cluster decomposes into supporting and opposing sub-arguments via the graph's standard pattern.
