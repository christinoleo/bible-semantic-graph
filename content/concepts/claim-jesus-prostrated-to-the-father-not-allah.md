The Gospels name the addressee of [[jesus]]'s Gethsemane prostration as the **Father** — Mark 14:36 *Abba, ho Patēr*; Matt 26:39, 42 *Páter mou*; Luke 22:42 *Páter* — a specific relational person within the Godhead, not the strict-unitarian [[allah]] of Islamic theology. The [[jesus-prostration-islamic-reading|Islamic reading]] silently substitutes Allah for the named Father, but the very texts it cites identify a filial addressee that is intelligible only within a [[trinity|Trinitarian]] frame.

The substitution is doubly incoherent on Islamic terms: Allah explicitly denies fatherhood in [[claim-q112-3-allah-does-not-beget|Q 112:3]] (*lam yalid wa lam yūlad*) and Q 19:35, so the destination of Jesus's prostration cannot be the Quranic Allah without contradicting the Quran itself.
