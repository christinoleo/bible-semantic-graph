[[gospel-of-mark|Mark]] 15:44-45 records [[pontius-pilate|Pilate]] himself doubting that [[jesus]] was already dead — *"and Pilate marveled that he was already dead, and summoning the centurion, he asked him whether he had been dead for some time"* (ESV) — then releasing the body only *after* receiving the centurion's confirmation. This is a Roman procedural certification of death by the officer responsible for the execution, recorded by a Gospel writer who has no motive to invent the doubt — the criterion of embarrassment cuts here too.

The Ahmadiyya thesis requires that the centurion was mistaken, deceived, or the entire account fabricated. Each alternative degrades the survival thesis's standing as a *historical* claim: the survival argument relies selectively on the Gospel record for the bare crucifixion event while requiring this specific Roman administrative detail to fail — exactly the selective-reliability pattern [[islamic-dilemma|the dilemma]] is built to expose.
