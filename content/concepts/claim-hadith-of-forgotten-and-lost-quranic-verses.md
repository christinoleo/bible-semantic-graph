Sunni hadith preserves multiple reports of Quranic material that was forgotten, accidentally omitted, or physically lost: [[muhammad]] forgetting verses until reminded by a companion's recitation (Bukhari 5032); [[aisha]]'s report (Ibn Majah 1944) that "the verse of stoning and of suckling an adult ten times were revealed, the parchment was under my bed, and when the Messenger of Allah died... a domestic sheep ate it." On the Sunni doctrine of *ḥadīth ṣaḥīḥ* these reports are authentic. They are incompatible with the "verbatim preserved Quran" claim used in Muslim apologetic against the [[bible]].
