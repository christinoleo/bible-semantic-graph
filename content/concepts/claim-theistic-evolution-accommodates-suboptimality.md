[[Theistic evolution]] — defended by Francis Collins, [[biologos|BioLogos]], and Denis Alexander — positively expects developmental constraints, contingent ancestry, and engineering tradeoffs as features of God's working through evolutionary processes, so [[claim-suboptimal-biology-refutes-design|suboptimality data]] does not weaken belief in the [[existence-of-god|God of classical theism]]. At most the [[argument-from-poor-design|poor-design argument]] refutes a narrow literalist [[young-earth-creationism|young-earth design model]], leaving the broader [[teleological-argument|theistic-design claim]] untouched.
