The standard academic rejoinder to the [[claim-lewis-trilemma-lord-liar-lunatic|Lewis Trilemma]]: the trilemma assumes Jesus actually made the high-Christology "I am God" claims the Gospels attribute to him, but historical-critical scholarship argues the explicit deity-claims are later Christological developments — the Synoptic Jesus does not say "I and the Father are one"; that comes from the Johannine corpus, dated later. If the deity-claims are evolved tradition rather than verbatim, the trilemma collapses into the missing fourth horn: *legend*. The Christian-apologetic counter relocates to [[claim-high-christology-in-earliest-strata|the earliness of high Christology]] in pre-Pauline material (Phil 2, 1 Cor 8:6, Col 1) — the legendary-development window cannot accommodate it.
