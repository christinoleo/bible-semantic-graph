The episode preserved in *[[sahih-al-bukhari|Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī]]* 3 (and parallel sirah literature) of [[muhammad|Muhammad]]'s first revelatory encounter in the Cave of Ḥirāʾ — being seized by an unseen being, commanded "Iqraʾ!" (Recite!), and returning home in terror to [[khadija]], whom he reportedly told he feared for his life and possibly his sanity. Khadija reassured him, then took him to her Christian cousin [[waraqa-ibn-nawfal|Waraqa ibn Nawfal]] who interpreted the experience as the Nāmūs (the angel) who had also come to Moses. Christian apologetics points to Muhammad's own initial reading — fear of demonic possession — as the more telling identification; Islamic tradition treats Waraqa's interpretation as confirming the angel [[gabriel]].
