Avery's ([[godlogic-apologist]]) Quranic counter to the [[claim-jesus-as-honorific-son-not-begotten|honorific-son escape]]: Surah Maryam Q 19:93, in the immediate context of the [[quran]]'s rejection of divine sonship, declares that *every* being in heaven and earth approaches the Most Merciful only as a *ʿabd* (servant/slave). The verse closes the rhetorical exit a Muslim interlocutor opens when they distinguish *honorific* from *begotten* sonship — by the Quran's own terms, no metaphorical filial register is admissible either; the only sanctioned relation to [[allah]] is servitude.
