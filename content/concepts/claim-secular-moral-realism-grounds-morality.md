Naturalist moral realists Erik Wielenberg and Russ Shafer-Landau argue that objective moral facts can exist as "brute" non-natural normative truths — irreducible to physical facts but not requiring divine grounding either. Wielenberg accepts the metaphysical commitment to "godless normative reality" without explaining moral facts via theism. The [[craig-moral-argument|theistic counter]] presses that such free-floating moral facts have no clear connection to the natural world and no mechanism by which moral properties supervene on physical properties, leaving them metaphysically more obscure than the theistic grounding.
