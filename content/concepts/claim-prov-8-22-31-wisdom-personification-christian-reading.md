Reads the personified Ḥokmāh of Prov 8:22-31 — present "before the deep," "*ʾāmôn*" (artisan/nursling) beside YHWH at creation — as a pre-incarnate manifestation of the divine [[trinity|Son]], picked up explicitly by the New Testament identification of [[jesus]] as the Wisdom of God (1 Cor 1:24, 30) and as the *logos di' hou ta panta egeneto* (John 1:1-3, Col 1:15-17). The argument's purchase is that the OT already personifies a divine agent who is both *with* YHWH and *of* YHWH — exactly the structural relation the doctrine of the Trinity later names *eternal generation*.
