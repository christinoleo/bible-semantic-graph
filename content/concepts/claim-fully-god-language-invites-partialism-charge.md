The Muslim debater's move (illustrated by [[muhammad-hijab]] in his exchange with the Christian apologist Avery): treat the Christian "[[jesus]] is fully God" as a quantitative claim, and press for the fraction — "How full? 90%? And what percent is the Father?" — pushing the orthodox formula into [[partialism]] and forcing the Christian to either retract *fully*, sound incoherent, or concede a heresy already rejected at [[nicaea-i|Nicaea]].
