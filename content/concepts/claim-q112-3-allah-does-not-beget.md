Surah al-Ikhlas (Q 112:3) confesses *lam yalid wa lam yūlad* — Allah "neither begets nor is begotten" — and the Quran repeatedly insists that the divine cannot stand in any filial relation (Q 19:35, Q 4:171, Q 19:88-92). On Islamic doctrine the very category of *Father* applied to God is a category mistake: Allah is strictly unitarian, beyond relation, and any reading that places Jesus in a son-of-God position to Allah is a doctrinal impossibility, not a debatable interpretation.

The claim functions in Islamic apologetics as the foundational disqualifier of [[trinity|Trinitarian]] readings of the Gospels: even granting the text says "Father," that name cannot refer to *Allah* — it would have to refer to something else, or be mistranslated, or be a [[tahrif|corrupted]] later overlay.
