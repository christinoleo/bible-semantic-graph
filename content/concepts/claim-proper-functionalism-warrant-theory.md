In the warrant trilogy (1993–2000), [[alvin-plantinga|Plantinga]] develops *proper functionalism*: a belief is *warranted* — the property that distinguishes true belief from knowledge — when it is produced by cognitive faculties functioning *properly* in the *epistemic environment* for which they were *designed*, according to a *design plan* successfully aimed at *truth*. The account is externalist (warrant does not require the believer's own access to it) and reliabilist (proper function is truth-tracking). Crucially, the "design plan" presupposes a designer — naturalistic accounts of cognitive function struggle to ground proper function, an asymmetry Plantinga deploys in [[claim-plantinga-eaan|the EAAN]] against naturalism.
