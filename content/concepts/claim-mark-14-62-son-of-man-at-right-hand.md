Asked by the high priest "*sy ei ho christos ho huios tou eulogētou* — are you the Christ, the Son of the Blessed?", [[jesus]] answers "*egō eimi* — I am," and pairs the divine-name saying with the visionary collocation of [[psalms|Ps]] 110:1 and Dan 7:13: "*opsesthe ton huion tou anthrōpou ek dexiōn kathēmenon tēs dynameōs kai erchomenon meta tōn nephelōn tou ouranou*." The Sanhedrin reads it as blasphemy and condemns him to death — the Markan Jesus thus claims, in the earliest Gospel, a divine identity seated at the Father's right hand, the same Father-Son distinction-in-unity that the doctrine of the [[trinity]] later formulates.
