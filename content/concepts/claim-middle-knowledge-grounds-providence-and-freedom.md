[[william-lane-craig|Craig]]'s argument: God's omniscience must include not just natural knowledge (all possibilities) and free knowledge (what He has decreed), but a *middle* category — knowledge of counterfactuals of creaturely freedom, i.e., what any creature *would* freely choose under any conditions. Armed with middle knowledge, God can sovereignly actualize the world that best fulfills His purposes *through* the genuinely free choices of creatures, dissolving the apparent contradiction between exhaustive divine providence and libertarian human freedom. The argument is offered as the metaphysically coherent alternative to Reformed compatibilism and to open theism.
