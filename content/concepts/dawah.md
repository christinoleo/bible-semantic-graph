Arabic *daʿwa* (call, invitation) — the Islamic activity of inviting non-Muslims to embrace Islam. In contemporary practice it ranges from informal personal conversation to organized comparative-religion polemic — most influentially the South African / Indian apologetic tradition of Ahmed Deedat (1918-2005) and his successor Zakir Naik, whose debate-style critiques of Christianity ([[claim-bible-has-different-versions-kjv-vs-niv|Bible-versions argument]], [[claim-pauline-corruption-of-jesus-teaching|Pauline corruption]], [[trinity]], [[aisha-marriage-age|age-of-Aisha defense]]) form the popular curriculum from which most lay-Muslim apologetic engagements with Christians draw.
