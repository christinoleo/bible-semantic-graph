The NT textual tradition is extraordinarily well-attested: [[p52]] dates within decades of composition, [[p46]] and thousands of further manuscripts precede the Quran's existence, and [[codex-vaticanus]] and [[codex-sinaiticus]] give complete 4th-century texts. Strong-[[tahrif]] — wholesale corruption of the NT before the 7th century — has no manuscript-evidential basis.
