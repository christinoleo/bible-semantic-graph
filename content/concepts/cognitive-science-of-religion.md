The interdisciplinary research program (Pascal Boyer, Scott Atran, Justin Barrett, Stewart Guthrie) that explains the cross-cultural prevalence of religious belief as a byproduct of evolved cognitive mechanisms — agency-detection (Hyperactive Agency Detection Device, HADD), theory-of-mind, theory of causation, attachment systems — selected for survival utility unrelated to the truth of religious propositions. The *debunking* deployment: if religious belief is fully explained by non-truth-tracking cognitive mechanisms, the *origin* of theistic belief provides no evidence of its *truth*. Christian responses press the [[claim-csr-is-genetic-fallacy|genetic fallacy]] (the cause of a belief is not its evidence) and note that some CSR researchers (Justin Barrett) are themselves Christian.
