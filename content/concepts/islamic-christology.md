The Quranic and post-Quranic Islamic account of [[jesus]] (Isa, عيسى). Treats Jesus with **unusually high regard** — virgin-born ([[quran]] 19:16-22), Messianic title (al-Masīḥ, Q 5:75), gifted with miracles including life from clay (Q 3:49), ascended to Allah (Q 4:158) — while categorically denying every claim that would make him divine or share the divine prerogative.

Three Quranic denials structure the position: (i) Jesus is not God or son of God (Q 5:72-73, 9:30) — to assert this is the gravest form of *shirk* (associating partners with Allah); (ii) the Trinity is rejected as veiled polytheism (Q 5:73); (iii) the crucifixion did not occur in fact — "they did not kill him, nor did they crucify him, but it was made to appear so to them" (Q 4:157), traditionally read as substitutionist (someone else was crucified) or docetist (only the appearance of death).

The governing principle is **Tawhīd** — absolute unicity of Allah, which logically forbids hypostatic union, eternal generation, or any account of incarnation. Classical kalam (Al-Ghazali, Ibn Hazm) engages [[chalcedonian-christology]] philosophically: the Trinity is internally incoherent on Aristotelian categories; Christology violates the Creator/creature divide. The argument is not historical but metaphysical.

Modern Islamic christology bifurcates. **Apologetic-polemical** strains ([[ahmed-deedat]], [[zakir-naik]]) sharpen the denial into the active claim that Christian sources have been *corrupted* ([[tahrif]]) — a thesis only weakly attested in pre-modern Islamic scholarship and hardened in 19th-20th-century anti-missionary contexts. **Sufi and dialogical** strains soften the contrast: Ibn Arabi's cosmology accommodates a high pneumatology under Islamic vocabulary; some modern Shi'a thinkers (e.g., Mahmoud Ayoub) treat the cross theologically while maintaining Quranic priority.

The interpretive tension within Islam itself is striking: Jesus is given more positive attributes in the Quran than any other prophet besides Muhammad, yet the doctrines Christians build *on* those attributes are categorically rejected. The classical settlement: Jesus is the sign and the messenger, never the message.
