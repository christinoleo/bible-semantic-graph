The atheist argument that biological organisms display features incompatible with an intelligent designer's craftsmanship: the recurrent laryngeal nerve of mammals (taking a wasteful detour around the aortic arch — in the giraffe spanning meters), the human retina wired backwards, vestigial organs (appendix, palmar grasp reflex, plantaris tendon), the panda's "thumb" (a wrist bone repurposed), and the universal blind-spot of the vertebrate eye. The features are precisely what *unguided* natural selection from constrained ancestral material would produce, and badly fit a designer optimizing from scratch — providing positive evidence *against* the [[teleological-argument|design argument]] and *for* common-descent evolution.
