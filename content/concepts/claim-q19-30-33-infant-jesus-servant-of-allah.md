In the cradle speech of Sūrat Maryam, the infant [[jesus]] says "*innī ʿabdu llāh* — I am the servant of Allah; He has given me the Scripture and made me a prophet" (Q 19:30). The Quran's first words from Jesus's own mouth define him as `ʿabd` (servant), not Son — directly framing his identity in Islamic, not Chalcedonian, categories.
