If a Muslim affirms that three printed [[quran|Qurʾāns]] — [[hafs-ibn-sulayman|Ḥafṣ]], Warsh, and Qālūn — are *"one Qurʾān"* despite being three textually distinct physical codices, they have already conceded the exact categorial structure the Christian needs for the [[trinity|Trinity]]: numerically *three* in one category (token codices / persons) yet *one* in another (the abstract scripture / the divine essence). The "1 + 1 + 1 ≠ 1" objection therefore equally indicts the Muslim's own bibliology, so consistent Islam either abandons the objection or abandons the unity of its scripture across the [[claim-qira-at-arabic-textual-variants|qirāʾāt]].
