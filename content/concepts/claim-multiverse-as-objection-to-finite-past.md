Eternal-inflation multiverse models — proposed by Andrei Linde, Alan Guth, and others — hypothesize a metaverse in which our observable universe is one of infinitely many bubble universes generated by ongoing inflation in a past-eternal substrate. If so, "the universe began to exist" applies only to *our* observable bubble, not to the metaverse, defusing the [[kalam-cosmological-argument|Kalām]]'s [[claim-universe-began-to-exist|second premise]]. The objection is itself refuted by the [[claim-big-bang-cosmology-supports-finite-past|BGV theorem]], which entails that even an inflating metaverse must have a past spacetime boundary.
