Every extant Greek manuscript of John reads παράκλητος; there is no manuscript witness to περικλυτός anywhere in the textual apparatus. The substitution required by the Islamic reading has zero textual attestation across [[p52]], [[p66]], [[p75]], [[codex-vaticanus]], [[codex-sinaiticus]], and the entire Byzantine tradition.
