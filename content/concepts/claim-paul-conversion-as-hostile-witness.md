[[paul]] persecuted the Jesus movement (Gal 1:13, 1 Cor 15:9) and then suddenly reversed, attributing the reversal to a personal appearance of the risen [[jesus]] (1 Cor 15:8). His pre-conversion hostility makes him a hostile witness — he had every motive *not* to invent the experience — and the transformation is dated within a few years of the crucifixion, too early for legendary development.
