The Reformed theological framework organizing salvation history around two (or three) overarching covenants — the *covenant of works* with [[adam|Adam]], the *covenant of grace* fulfilled in [[jesus|Christ]], and (in some formulations) a *covenant of redemption* among the persons of the [[trinity|Trinity]]. Sharply distinguishes from [[dispensationalism]] by holding the [[new-testament|New Covenant]] church as continuous with Israel as the one people of God, rather than as a parenthetical body distinct from Israel. Articulated by Reformed scholastics (Johannes Cocceius, Hermann Witsius) and confessionalized in the Westminster Standards (1646-1649).
