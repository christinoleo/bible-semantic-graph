[[daniel-9]]'s Seventy Weeks conclude with the Maccabean crisis: the "anointed one cut off" (9:26) is the High Priest Onias III, deposed and murdered in 171 BCE, and "the prince who is to come" who profanes the temple is [[antiochus-iv-epiphanes]]. [[book-of-daniel]] was composed under that persecution, and the messianic-Jesus chronology requires a numerical contrivance to reach 30 CE.
