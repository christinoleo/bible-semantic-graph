The Islamic doctrinal framework governing prophethood (*nubuwwa*): a linear succession of messengers sent by Allah to every nation calling them to [[tawhid]], numbering 124,000 by tradition with 25 named in the [[quran]] — from [[adam]] through [[noah]], [[abraham]], [[moses]], and [[jesus]] (Isa), culminating in [[muhammad]] as *Khatam an-Nabiyyin*, the Seal of the Prophets (Q 33:40). Prophets share *ʿiṣmah* — divinely guaranteed inviolability from grave sin and, in Sunni majority view, from being killed by their enemies in mission ([[claim-prophet-inviolability-isma]]). The doctrine flattens [[jesus]] into the prophetic line: penultimate in dignity, never divine, his message identical in substance to Muhammad's and corrupted by his followers via [[tahrif]]. It is the structural premise behind [[jesus-was-a-muslim-prophet]].
