An infinite series cannot be formed by successive addition: each new moment is finite in distance from any prior moment, so however far back the series extends, the current moment is reached by traversing only finite intervals. If the past were *actually* infinite, the present would have been reached by traversing an actual infinite — which is impossible. Therefore the past is finite, and the [[claim-universe-began-to-exist|universe began to exist]]. Distinct in argumentative structure from [[claim-impossibility-of-actual-infinite|Hilbert's Hotel]]: this argument concerns *temporal becoming*, that concerns *the actual infinite simpliciter*.
