If the metaverse contains a vast (or infinite) ensemble of universes with varying physical constants, then by the [[claim-anthropic-principle-explains-fine-tuning|anthropic principle]] we must necessarily find ourselves in a life-permitting universe; the observed "fine-tuning" is then a selection effect, not evidence of design. Defended by Leonard Susskind, Steven Weinberg, Max Tegmark. The argument is refuted by the [[claim-multiverse-itself-requires-fine-tuning|regress problem]] — the metaverse-generator itself appears finely tuned.
