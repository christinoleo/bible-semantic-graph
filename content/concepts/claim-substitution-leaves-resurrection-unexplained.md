Substitutionist readings (Judas, Simon, or another stand-in on the cross) leave the post-crucifixion resurrection narratives unexplained: the disciples saw, spoke with, ate with, and recognized [[jesus]] himself, not a substitute. The Islamic reading must either deny these encounters as further [[tahrif]] or absorb the apologetic cost of a [[jesus]] who appeared alive without having died.
