In the earliest Gospel the three persons appear together at the Jordan: the heavens are torn (*schizomenous*), the Spirit descends *eis auton* into [[jesus]], and the Father's voice declares "*sy ei ho huios mou ho agapētos* — you are my beloved Son." That [[gospel-of-mark|Mark]] — by scholarly consensus the earliest written Gospel — opens his narrative with this triadic disclosure is read as evidence that triadic monotheism is not a late doctrinal overlay but is present at the foundation of Christian witness to [[jesus]].
