High Christology appears in the earliest layers of NT material: the [[philippians]] 2:6-11 hymn, the binitarian formula of [[1-corinthians]] 8:6, and the [[colossians]] 1:15-20 hymn are pre-Pauline traditions datable to within ~20 years of the crucifixion. They predate any plausible window for a Pauline "corruption" of the original [[jesus]] teaching.
