[[arius|Arius]]'s flagship proof-text reads the LXX of Prov 8:22 — "*kyrios ektisen me archēn hodōn autou* — the Lord *created* me as the beginning of his ways" — as decisively predicating *creaturehood* of personified Wisdom (and therefore of the pre-incarnate Son the [[claim-prov-8-22-31-wisdom-personification-christian-reading|Christian reading]] identifies with her), against [[trinity|Nicene]] *homoousios*. Athanasius's response (*Orations Against the Arians* II) reads *ektisen* metaphorically of the eternal generation, or refers it specifically to the incarnation rather than to the Son's eternal being — preserving the identification of Wisdom with the Son without conceding her creaturehood. The verse is the locus where the fourth-century Trinitarian controversy is most directly a controversy of LXX exegesis.
