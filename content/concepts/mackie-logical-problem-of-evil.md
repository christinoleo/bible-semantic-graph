[[j-l-mackie|J. L. Mackie]] (1955, *Evil and Omnipotence*): the conjunction "God is omnipotent," "God is wholly good," and "evil exists" is *strictly logically inconsistent*, so at least one classical theistic claim must be false. The challenge is deductive, not probabilistic — no possible world reconciles the three.
