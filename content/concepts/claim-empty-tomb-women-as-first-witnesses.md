All four canonical gospels independently place women — [[mary-magdalene]] first — as the discoverers of the empty tomb, in a legal-cultural context (first-century Judea) where female testimony was generally not admissible. An invented account would have placed male disciples there; the unanimous, embarrassing detail is best explained by historical memory.
