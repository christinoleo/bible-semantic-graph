The values of the fundamental physical constants — cosmological constant (Λ ≈ 10⁻¹²², tuned to 1 part in 10¹²⁰), gravitational coupling, strong-nuclear coupling, ratio of electron to proton mass, low initial entropy of the universe (Penrose's ~1 in 10^10^123) — lie within extraordinarily narrow ranges compatible with the formation of stable atoms, stars, galaxies, and chemistry-supporting life. Tiny changes in any of dozens of independent parameters would render the universe sterile. The observation grounds the [[fine-tuning-argument|fine-tuning argument]] for design.
