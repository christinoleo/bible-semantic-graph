The [[muhammad-hijab|Hijab]] move: Q 50:38's "no weariness (*lughūb*) touched Us" is the Qurʾān correcting the Hebrew Bible's anthropomorphic rest narrative — [[genesis]] 2:2-3 has God *resting* on the seventh day, and Exo 31:17 sharpens this with the verb *nafash* ("was refreshed / drew breath"), which classical rabbinic commentary takes as a literal divine rest. The Qurʾān, on this reading, refuses the implication that God could be tired — a paradigm case of the [[muhaymin|muhaymin-as-corrector]] function.
