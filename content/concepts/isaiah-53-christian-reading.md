The Suffering Servant of [[isaiah-53]] prefigures the vicarious atoning death of [[jesus]] as the [[messiah]]: the Servant bears guilt that is not his own ("the iniquity of us all", 53:6), suffers silently, and is "cut off from the land of the living" (53:8). Substitutionary suffering of one *for* the many individuates the Servant against any corporate-Israel reading, since Israel cannot vicariously atone for itself.
