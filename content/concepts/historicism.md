The interpretive position that [[revelation|Revelation]] charts a continuous symbolic timeline from the apostolic age to the *parousia*, with each seal, trumpet, and bowl corresponding to specific events or epochs of church history. The dominant Protestant reading from the Reformation (Luther, Calvin, the Westminster Divines) through the nineteenth century, typically identifying the [[antichrist|antichrist]] / Beast with the Papacy. Largely displaced by [[futurism]] and [[preterism]] in modern scholarship.
