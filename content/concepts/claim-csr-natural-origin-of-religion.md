Cognitive Science of Religion explains the cross-cultural prevalence of belief in God(s) as the byproduct of evolved cognitive mechanisms with non-religious functions: the Hyperactive Agency Detection Device (HADD) — selected because false positives for predators are cheap, false negatives lethal — generates intuitions of *agent* explanations for ambiguous natural events. Theory-of-mind combined with HADD generates "minds behind everything." On the debunking deployment: if theistic intuitions are produced by mechanisms not tracking the truth of religious propositions, the prevalence of belief in God provides no evidence of God's existence. Refuted by [[claim-csr-is-genetic-fallacy|the genetic fallacy response]].
