The cumulative Islamic argumentative program against the creedal claims of [[christianity]], built on a [[quran]]ic foundation (Q 4:171, 5:72–75, 9:30) and elaborated across three layers: classical [[classical-kalam|kalam]] philosophical objections to the [[trinity]] and incarnation (Ibn Hazm, Al-Ghazali, Ibn Taymiyya), the textual-corruption thesis of [[tahrif]] hardened in 19th–20th century anti-missionary contexts, and modern televised polemic ([[ahmed-deedat]], [[zakir-naik]]) that fuses both streams for mass audiences. The program treats [[jesus]] with high prophetic regard while rejecting every doctrine Christians build on him, governed throughout by [[tawhid]] as non-negotiable axiom.
