Islamic theology rejects the [[trinity]] as incompatible with *[[tawhid|tawḥīd]]* — the absolute unicity of God ([[quran|Q]]112): God is one, has no associates ([[shirk]]), no son, no partner. Q4:171 explicitly enjoins Christians to "desist" from saying "three" and to affirm Allah is one God; Q5:73 declares unbelievers those who say "Allah is the third of three." On the Islamic reading, the Trinity is therefore either tritheism or incoherent — and in either case excluded from the strict monotheism the [[quran]] insists is shared between Abrahamic faiths.
