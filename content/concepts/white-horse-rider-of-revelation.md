Two riders on white horses appear in [[revelation|Revelation]]: in Rev 6:2 a crowned figure given a bow who "came out conquering, and to conquer" (first of the four horsemen of the apocalypse, opening the seven seals), and in Rev 19:11-16 [[jesus]] himself, "Faithful and True," eyes of fire, robe dipped in blood, returning in judgment to defeat the [[antichrist|Beast]]. Christian interpreters split on the Rev 6:2 figure: some identify him with Christ (parallel to Rev 19), others — especially in dispensationalist and futurist readings — identify him as the antichrist's *imitation conquest*, a counterfeit messiah riding the white horse to inaugurate the end-times deception under appearance of peace and victory.
