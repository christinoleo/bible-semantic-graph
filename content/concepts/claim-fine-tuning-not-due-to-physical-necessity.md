No known principle of physics forces the fundamental constants to take their observed values — they appear in the equations as free parameters fitted by measurement, not derived from theory. A future "Theory of Everything" might fix some constants, but any such theory would have its own free parameters at a higher level, simply relocating the contingency. The "necessity" disjunct of the [[fine-tuning-argument|fine-tuning trilemma]] (necessity / chance / design) is therefore not currently supported.
