The Ahmadiyya interpretation holds that [[jesus]] was placed on the cross but did not die — he was taken down alive in a swoon, recovered, and migrated east to Kashmir, where he eventually died a natural death and was buried at the Roza Bal shrine. Q 4:157's denial is read as denying his *death* on the cross, not the bare fact of being crucified.
