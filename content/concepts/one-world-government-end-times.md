Both Islamic and Christian eschatologies feature a global end-times political consolidation: in Islam, the [[mahdi|Mahdī]] establishes a worldwide caliphate ruled by Islamic law before [[returning-isa-islamic-eschatology|ʿĪsā]] descends; in Christian apocalyptic, the [[antichrist|Beast]] of [[revelation|Rev]] 13:7 is given "authority over every tribe and people and language and nation," and the fourth beast of [[book-of-daniel|Dan]] 7:23 "shall devour the whole earth." The structural similarity — a Middle-Eastern-centered global authority demanding universal submission, with capital punishment for refusal ([[revelation|Rev]] 13:15, 20:4 — beheading; Islamic *ridda* law — execution for apostasy) — fuels the [[claim-islamic-mahdi-fits-biblical-antichrist-profile|Islamic-antichrist thesis]] in Christian apologetics.
