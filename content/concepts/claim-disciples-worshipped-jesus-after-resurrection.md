In Matt 28:9 and 28:17 the women and the Eleven *worship* (*prosekynēsan*) the risen [[jesus]] with no rebuke; in John 20:28 Thomas confesses "*ho kyrios mou kai ho theos mou* — my Lord and my God," and Jesus receives the confession with a blessing. Compare Acts 10:25-26, where Peter *forbids* Cornelius's prostration — the Gospel pattern of Jesus accepting what Peter rejects is itself the textual marker of divine claim.
