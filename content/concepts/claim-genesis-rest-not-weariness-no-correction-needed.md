The Christian reply: Hebrew *šābat* (Gen 2:2-3) denotes *ceasing* from work, not exhaustion, and the Exo 31:17 *nafash* belongs to anthropomorphic ritual language (paralleled by God's "smelling" Noah's offering, "hearing" prayer, "walking" in Eden) — not a metaphysical claim that the omnipotent creator was tired. Q 50:38, therefore, corrects a misreading of [[genesis]] that the Hebrew text never required; nothing is being *corrected* in the scripture itself.
