*[[sahih-al-bukhari|Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī]]* 6130 records [[aisha|ʿĀʾisha]] narrating that she played with dolls in [[muhammad|Muhammad]]'s presence and her friends would hide when he entered, with the explicit editorial note that playing with dolls was permitted *because she had not yet reached puberty*. This text — narrated by ʿĀʾisha herself in the most authoritative Sunni hadith collection — directly contradicts the apologetic claim that she had already attained physical maturity at the time of the marriage's consummation.
