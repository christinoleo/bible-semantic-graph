Peter van Inwagen's modal "happy fault" response to the [[rowe-evidential-problem-of-evil|evidential problem of evil]]: a world containing the [[fall-of-man|Fall]] together with [[incarnation|Incarnation]] and [[atonement|redemption]] is plausibly *better* than any world with no Fall and no need of a Redeemer, so it is intelligible that God permits horrendous evil for the sake of overwhelmingly greater goods unavailable otherwise. The argument needs only modal possibility — not actual demonstration of those goods — to neutralize the inference from apparently gratuitous suffering to God's nonexistence.
