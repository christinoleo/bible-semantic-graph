The pre-Pauline hymn of [[philippians]] 2:6-11 frames the incarnate Son as one who, "*en morphē theou hyparchōn* — being in the form of God," *did not consider equality with God a thing to be grasped*, but emptied himself (*ekenōsen*) and took the form of a servant. Submission and prayer are the *content* of [[kenosis]], not evidence against divinity.
