[[paul]]'s "received and delivered" formula in [[1-corinthians]] 15:3-8 — "Christ died for our sins... was buried... was raised" — is recognized across the critical-scholarly spectrum as a pre-Pauline creed datable to within 3-5 years of the crucifixion. This collapses the timeline available for the kind of legendary corruption that [[tahrif]] would require.
