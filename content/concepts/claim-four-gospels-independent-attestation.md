All four canonical Gospels — [[gospel-of-matthew]], [[gospel-of-mark]], [[gospel-of-luke]], and the independent Johannine tradition in [[gospel-of-john]] — narrate the crucifixion of [[jesus]] with distinctive material. This satisfies the historian's criterion of multiple attestation across independent source streams.
