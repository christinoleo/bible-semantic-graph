The Islamic doctrine that [[muhammad]] is *uswa ḥasana* — a perfect moral exemplar (Q33:21) — together with the contested historical record of his conduct preserved in the Sirah and Hadith literature. Christian apologetics challenges this doctrine through specific historical episodes ([[aisha-marriage-age|Aisha's marriage age]], the Banu Qurayza massacre, the marriage to Zaynab bint Jahsh, [[prophetic-privilege-in-islam|self-exempting revelations]]). Islamic responses appeal either to cultural-historical context, to alternative readings of the source material, or to the *tu quoque* observation that biblical prophets also sinned.
