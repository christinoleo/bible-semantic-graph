[[john-hick]] (*Evil and the God of Love*, 1966), retrieving [[irenaeus-of-lyons|Irenaean]] strands against the [[augustinian-theodicy|Augustinian]] line: evil and suffering are necessary preconditions for genuine moral and spiritual growth, and God establishes an "epistemic distance" within which creatures freely mature into the divine likeness through encounter with hardship. The world is a *vale of soul-making*, not a failed paradise.
