The Islamic apologetic claim that [[mary-mother-of-jesus|Mary the mother of Jesus]] was a young teenager (often asserted at 12–14) when she conceived [[jesus|Jesus]], thus establishing that the dominant Christian tradition itself centers on a young bride — making [[muhammad|Muhammad]]'s marriage to [[aisha|ʿĀʾisha]] at six and consummation at nine a difference of degree rather than kind. The argument requires that Christianity grant the historical parallel and so be silenced as a critic.
