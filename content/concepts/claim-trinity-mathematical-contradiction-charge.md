The popular polemical objection — standard in Ahmed Deedat-style [[dawah|daʿwa]] — that the [[trinity|Trinity]] equates 1 + 1 + 1 to 1, which is mathematically incoherent. If Father is God, Son is God, Holy Spirit is God, and there is one God, then either the three are not really distinct, or there are three Gods after all. Refuted on Christian grounds by the [[claim-trinity-not-mathematical-contradiction|essence-persons distinction]]: the formula is one *essence* (*ousia*) in three *persons* (*hypostases*) — distinct ontological categories, not three numerically identical things forced into one.
