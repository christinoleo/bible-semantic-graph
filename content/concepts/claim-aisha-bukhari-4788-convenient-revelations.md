*[[sahih-al-bukhari|Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī]]* 4788 records [[aisha|ʿĀʾisha]]'s remark to [[muhammad|Muhammad]] in the context of a revelation accommodating his marital affairs: *"I feel that your Lord hastens in fulfilling your wishes and desires."* The observation — preserved in the most authoritative Sunni hadith collection and attributed to his own wife — registers from within the prophet's household the same pattern Christian apologetics points to externally: that revelations consistently arrived precisely when needed to legitimize personal preferences.
