The "Seventy Weeks" oracle in [[book-of-daniel]] 9:24–27, in which the angel Gabriel announces a seventy-sevens period decreed for Jerusalem and the people, terminating in the coming and "cutting off" of an anointed one (*mashiach nagid*) and the destruction of the city and sanctuary. Christian readers since the Patristic era have treated the chronology as terminating in [[jesus]] and the 70 CE destruction of the Second Temple — making Daniel a dated prediction of the Messiah's arrival. [[jewish-anti-missionary]] readers ([[tovia-singer]]) reckon the seventy weeks differently, ending the count at the Maccabean crisis and the death of the high priest Onias III, or at the 70 CE destruction itself with no messianic referent. Critical scholarship treats the oracle as a *vaticinium ex eventu* composed during the Antiochene persecution of the 160s BCE. Three rival chronologies, three rival anointed-ones.
