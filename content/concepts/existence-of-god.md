The central question of classical [[natural-theology]]: whether a being identified as God exists. The thesis is contested by [[atheism]] and defended by a cluster of arguments — cosmological, teleological, moral, ontological, and from consciousness — each engaging the question from a different premise. The arguments do not stand or fall together; each can be evaluated on its own merits and is typically advanced as cumulative case rather than single proof.
