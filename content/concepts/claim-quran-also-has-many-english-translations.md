The [[quran]] is available in dozens of English translations — Pickthall, Yusuf Ali, Arberry, Muhsin Khan, Sahih International, The Clear Quran — that differ in word choice, sentence structure, and theological emphasis in exactly the same way the KJV and NIV differ in English. If translation diversity disqualifies the Bible, it disqualifies the Quran on the same principle; the argument cannot be wielded selectively against Christian scripture.
