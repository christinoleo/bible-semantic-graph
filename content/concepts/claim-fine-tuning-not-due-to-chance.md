The "chance" disjunct of the [[fine-tuning-argument|trilemma]] holds that the life-permitting values are a brute lottery winner. But the relevant probabilities are extraordinary: the cosmological constant alone is tuned to 1 part in 10¹²⁰, and a dozen+ independent parameters multiply these improbabilities. "Chance" therefore requires either a sampling mechanism (which is the [[claim-multiverse-hypothesis-explains-fine-tuning|multiverse hypothesis]]) or a leap of faith comparable to attributing complex specified arrangements to random arrangement — which on any other domain would be rejected as inferential abuse.
