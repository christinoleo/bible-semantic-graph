The Muslim reply (here voiced by [[hamza-of-hamzas-den]]): [[muhammad]] never claims to *follow* [[jesus]]'s teaching the way [[paul-of-tarsus|Paul]] claimed to — [[allah]] is the one who confirms previous prophets, through Muhammad's recitation — and [[progressive-sharia|each prophet has his own sharia]], so the [[quran]] can introduce rulings ([[hajj]], dietary laws) absent from Jesus's teaching without contradiction. The [[claim-muhammad-confirms-jesus-so-cannot-contradict-him|"confirmation excludes contradiction"]] Christian move treats confirmation more strongly than the Quranic doctrine itself requires.
