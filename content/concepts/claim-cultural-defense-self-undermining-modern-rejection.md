The cultural-relativist defense ("times were different") is consistently paired by the same Muslim defenders with the admission that they would *not* accept such a marriage today and that the practice is now wrong. But the *only* reason the practice is granted historical immunity is that [[muhammad|Muhammad]] performed it — which means the moral judgment is not actually being suspended on cultural-historical grounds; it is being suspended *specifically and only* to protect Muhammad's status as moral exemplar (*uswa ḥasana*, Q33:21). That is a confession that the action is wrong on the defender's own moral lights, salvaged only by special pleading for the prophet.
