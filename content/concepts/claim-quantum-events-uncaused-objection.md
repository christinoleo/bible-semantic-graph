Quantum mechanics describes uncaused events — virtual-particle pair-creation in the vacuum, radioactive decay, wave-function collapse — which (on common interpretations) lack determining causes for their specific outcomes. If such events are genuinely uncaused, the [[claim-causal-principle|causal principle]] "everything that begins to exist has a cause" is empirically falsified, and the [[kalam-cosmological-argument|Kalām]]'s first premise collapses. Defended by Quentin Smith, Lawrence Krauss, Stephen Hawking. Christian responses distinguish *indeterminate* causation from *uncaused* events, and note that the Copenhagen interpretation is not the only viable quantum interpretation.
