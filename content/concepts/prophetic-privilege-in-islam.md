The category of *khaṣāʾiṣ al-nabī* — Quranic revelations and legal rulings specific to [[muhammad]] and not binding on other Muslim men. The most disputed include the exemption from the four-wife limit (Q33:50, [[claim-muhammad-self-exempting-revelations-surah-33|Surah Al-Aḥzāb]]), the prohibition on his widows remarrying (Q33:53), and the prompt timing of revelations addressing personal disputes — observed pointedly by [[aisha|ʿĀʾisha]] herself ([[claim-aisha-bukhari-4788-convenient-revelations|Bukhari 4788]]). Christian apologetics treats this category as evidence of authored convenience; Islamic apologetics treats it as the legitimate prerogative of the final prophet.
