The interpretive position that the bulk of [[revelation|Revelation]] was fulfilled in the first century — typically in the events leading to the destruction of Jerusalem in 70 CE — with the [[antichrist|Beast]] identified as the emperor Nero (the Hebrew gematria of *Nerōn Qesar* yielding 666). *Partial preterism* (R.C. Sproul, Kenneth Gentry) holds the *parousia* still future; *full preterism* holds all biblical prophecy fulfilled by 70 CE.
