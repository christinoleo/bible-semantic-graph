The Islamic apologetic argument that the existence of multiple English Bible translations (King James Version, New International Version, ESV, etc.) which "say different things" demonstrates that the Christian scripture has been altered, corrupted, or is unreliable — in contrast to the [[quran]], which the argument claims has remained verbatim-unchanged via the memorization (*ḥifẓ*) tradition. This is one of the most common popular-level [[dawah]] arguments against biblical reliability.
