The third person of the [[trinity|Trinity]] in Christian theology — proceeding from the Father (and, in Western theology following the *Filioque*, also from the Son), of one essence with the Father and the Son, fully divine and personal. Active in creation (Gen 1:2), prophecy (Num 11, Ezek 36-37), the conception and ministry of [[jesus|Jesus]] (Lk 1:35, Mk 1:10), and the formation and ongoing life of the church (Acts 2, [[pauline-epistles|1 Cor 12]]). Identified with the *Parakletos* of [[gospel-of-john|Jn]] 14:16, 14:26 — a passage Islamic apologetics ([[paraclete-as-muhammad-argument|Paraclete-as-Muhammad]]) reinterprets to designate [[muhammad|Muhammad]] himself.
