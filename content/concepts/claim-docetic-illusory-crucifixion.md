A docetic-adjacent Islamic reading holds that Allah raised [[jesus]] bodily to heaven before the cross ([[quran]] 4:158, *rafaʿahu Allāhu ilayhi*), and the apparent crucifixion was an illusion projected onto the witnesses. The cross is real to the eye but unreal in fact — [[jesus]] never suffered it, paralleling though not identical to the [[docetic-christology]] of Gnostic milieus.
