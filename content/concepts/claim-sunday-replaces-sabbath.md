The Sabbath obligation of the Decalogue is transferred to Sunday — the Lord's Day (*kyriakē hēmera*, Rev 1:10) — by apostolic authority and Christ's lordship over the Sabbath (Mark 2:28). The earliest church gathered on the first day of the week (Acts 20:7, 1 Cor 16:2); the Didache and Ignatius confirm 2nd-century practice. The day shifts; the obligation persists. This is the mainstream Catholic catechetical and Reformed-confessional position (Westminster Confession 21.7, *Catechism of the Catholic Church* 2175-2176).

Against the Islamic argument: Christians did not abandon the Sabbath principle; they relocated it under the authority of [[jesus]] himself.
