The Gospel and Pauline texts witnessing to the crucifixion of [[jesus]] are preserved in [[p52]], [[p46]], [[codex-vaticanus]], and [[codex-sinaiticus]] with high textual stability across thousands of witnesses. There is no manuscript evidence for any pre-corruption tradition disagreeing with the canonical record — [[tahrif]] on this point is an unfalsifiable assertion without textual footprint.
