The position that the [[second-coming-of-christ|return of Christ]] occurs *after* a literal thousand-year (or symbolic long) period of progressive Christianization of the world through the church's mission — the kingdom advances victoriously *before* Christ's return rather than awaiting catastrophic intervention. Dominant in 18th–19th century Anglo-American Protestantism (Jonathan Edwards, Charles Hodge, B.B. Warfield); revived in the 20th century through Christian Reconstructionism (Rousas Rushdoony, Greg Bahnsen).
