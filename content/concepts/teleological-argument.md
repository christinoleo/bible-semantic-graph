The family of arguments inferring a designing intelligence behind the cosmos from observed order, purpose, or improbable specified arrangement. Classically expressed by William Paley's watchmaker analogy, the argument was reformulated in the late twentieth century around the [[fine-tuning-argument|fine-tuning]] of physical constants — that the values permitting life lie within an extraordinarily narrow band of an otherwise life-hostile parameter space. Intelligent Design as a sub-tradition extends the argument to biological information.
