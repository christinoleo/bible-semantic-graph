The same narrative in Ibn Hisham's *Sīrah* preserves that after the chest-splitting incident, [[halima|Halīma]] and her husband Ḥārith were so disturbed by the unexplained events — including this one — that they returned the young [[muhammad|Muhammad]] to his family in Mecca, *cutting short the normal fostering arrangement*. The detail survives despite being awkward for later Islamic veneration: the prophet's own foster-mother judged something frightening had happened that she could not protect him from. The criterion of embarrassment supports the historical core while leaving the supernatural framing as later interpretation.
