Throughout the Gospels [[jesus]] addresses God in the second person — "Our Father" (Matt 6:9), "Abba, Father" (Mark 14:36), "Father, the hour has come" (John 17) — placing himself in the structural role of worshipper, not the worshipped. A being who *prays to* God cannot, on the Islamic reading, simultaneously *be* the God he prays to.
