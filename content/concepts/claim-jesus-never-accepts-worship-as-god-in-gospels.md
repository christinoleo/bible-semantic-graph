In the canonical Gospels no scene presents [[jesus]] accepting *proskynesis* under the explicit category of "worship-of-God-himself" — the kneeling and bowing he receives is read in Islamic apologetic as homage to a messianic-king figure, not adoration of deity. The category-conflation between royal and divine *proskynesis* is, on this reading, a later Hellenistic-Christian imposition.
