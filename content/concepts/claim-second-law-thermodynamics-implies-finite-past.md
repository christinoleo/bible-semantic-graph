The Second Law of Thermodynamics requires that the entropy of a closed system increase over time toward an equilibrium "heat death." If the universe had existed for an infinite past, it would already have reached maximum entropy — but the observed universe is conspicuously far from heat death, with usable energy and ordered structure. Therefore the universe has a finite past. The argument complements the [[claim-impossibility-of-actual-infinite|philosophical]] and [[claim-big-bang-cosmology-supports-finite-past|cosmological]] arguments for the same conclusion.
