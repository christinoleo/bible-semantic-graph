The Decalogue commands Sabbath on the seventh day (Exod 20:8-11); [[jesus]] kept it (Luke 4:16); Christians shifted their main day of corporate worship to Sunday. The argument: this shift is a betrayal of [[jesus]]'s practice and the Mosaic command he affirmed (Matt 5:17 — "I have not come to abolish the Law"). Muslims, while not Saturday-keepers in the Christian sense, at minimum did not invent a new sacred day in defiance of Jesus's Sabbath observance.

The argument is deployed less to claim that Muslims keep Sabbath (Friday's *jumʿah* is a congregational prayer, not Sabbath rest) than to demonstrate a *contradiction inside Christian practice* — the more familiar the Christian is with their own scripture, the sharper the discomfort.
