Religion of the Jewish people, centered on covenantal relationship with YHWH, the Hebrew Bible ([[tanakh]]), and post-biblical rabbinic literature — Mishnah, the Babylonian and Jerusalem Talmuds, and the Midrashim. Spans [[rabbinic]], Karaite, Reform, Conservative, Orthodox, and Reconstructionist branches, each with distinct postures toward halakha, scriptural authority, and modernity. Stands in long scriptural and polemical relation to [[christianity]] (shared Hebrew Bible, contested messianic reading) and to [[islam]] (shared Abrahamic frame, contested prophetic succession). This Node is the religion frame; the corresponding anti-missionary apologetic tradition is [[jewish-anti-missionary]].
