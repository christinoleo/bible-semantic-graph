Luke 23:34a — "Father, forgive them, for they do not know what they do" — is absent from several major early witnesses (𝔓⁷⁵, Sinaiticus by the first hand, Vaticanus, Bezae corrected, the Sahidic, and others) but present in the Majority Text and most modern translations (typically in brackets or with a marginal note). The question is whether [[gospel-of-luke|Luke]] originally included it (later omitted by some scribes — possibly out of post-70-CE anti-Jewish sentiment refusing intercession for Jerusalem) or whether it is a later harmonizing insertion. Either reading is doctrinally neutral; even at minimum, [[jesus]]'s prayer of forgiveness from the cross is consonant with Lukan theology elsewhere (Acts 7:60, Stephen's parallel prayer).
