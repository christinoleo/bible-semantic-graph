Arabic *al-Furqān* (from the root *f-r-q*, "to separate, distinguish") — the *criterion* by which truth is discriminated from falsehood. The [[quran]] applies the term variably: to the [[torah]] given to [[moses]] and Aaron (Q 21:48, Q 2:53), to the Qurʾān itself (Q 3:4, Q 25:1), and to general moral discernment granted to the believer (Q 8:29). The Qurʾānic predication of *al-Furqān* of the Torah-of-Moses is regularly mobilized in Christian apologetic ([[claim-quran-calls-torah-al-furqan-refutes-tahrif]]) as a refutation of strong-form [[tahrif]]: the Qurʾān cannot coherently call the Torah a *criterion* for distinguishing truth while also holding that the Torah was textually corrupted before the time of [[muhammad]].
