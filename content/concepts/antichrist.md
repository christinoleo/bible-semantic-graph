The biblical end-times figure described as: "the antichrist" who denies the Father and the Son (1 Jn 2:22, 4:3, 2 Jn 7); "the man of lawlessness" who exalts himself above every so-called god and demands worship in the temple ([[2-thessalonians|2 Thess]] 2:3-4); the "Beast" with global authority who blasphemes God, makes war on the saints, beheads dissenters, and demands universal allegiance ([[revelation|Rev]] 13, 20:4); the "little horn" of [[book-of-daniel|Dan]] 7:8-25 who speaks against the Most High and changes times and law. Christian eschatological traditions differ on whether the antichrist is a single end-times figure (futurism), a succession of figures (historicism), or a present spirit / type (idealism, preterism).
