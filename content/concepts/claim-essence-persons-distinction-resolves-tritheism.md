The patristic resolution: God is *one in essence* (*una substantia* / *mia ousia*) and *three in persons* (*tres personae* / *treis hypostaseis*) — articulated by [[tertullian]] for the Latin West and by the Cappadocian Fathers ([[basil-the-great|Basil]], [[gregory-of-nazianzus]], [[gregory-of-nyssa]]) for the Greek East. Essence answers *what God is* (the divine nature, single and indivisible); person answers *who* — three distinct subjects of relations of origin (paternity, generation, procession). The categories are not "1+1+1=1" in any single dimension; "three" and "one" answer different questions, so no contradiction arises.
