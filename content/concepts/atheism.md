The position that God does not exist (strong / positive atheism) or that there is no sufficient reason to believe God exists (weak / negative atheism). Distinct from agnosticism, which suspends the question. In contemporary discourse, the "[[new-atheism|New Atheist]]" movement (Dawkins, Hitchens, Harris, Dennett) reframed the debate evidentially, prompting renewed defenses of classical theistic arguments by analytic philosophers of religion.
