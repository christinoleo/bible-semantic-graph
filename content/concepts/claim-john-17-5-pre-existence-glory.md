[[jesus]] prays in John 17:5, "Father, glorify me in your own presence with the glory I had with you *before the world existed* (*pro tou ton kosmon einai*)." Pre-existent glory shared with the Father is a divine attribute and cannot, in classical theism, be predicated of a created prophet.
