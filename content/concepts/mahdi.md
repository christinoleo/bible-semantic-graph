In Islamic eschatology, the Mahdi is the awaited righteous leader from the lineage of [[muhammad|Muhammad]] (through Fāṭima) who will rise in the end times, unite the Muslim *umma* under a global caliphate, conquer the earth, and rule justly before — in Sunni belief — being joined by the returning [[returning-isa-islamic-eschatology|ʿĪsā]] to defeat [[dajjal|al-Dajjāl]]. In Twelver Shīʿism the Mahdi is specifically the Twelfth Imam, [[muhammad-al-mahdi|Muhammad al-Mahdī]] (b. 869 CE), believed to be in occultation and awaited. Primary hadith sources include *[[sunan-abu-dawud|Sunan Abī Dāwūd]]* 4282-4287 and parts of *[[sahih-muslim|Muslim]]*. The Quran does not directly mention the Mahdi; the figure is constructed entirely from hadith.
