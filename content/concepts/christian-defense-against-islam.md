The Christian counter-program answering [[islamic-critique-of-christianity]], whose lineage runs from [[john-of-damascus]]'s *De Haeresibus* §100 (8th c., the first systematic Christian engagement, treating Islam as a Christological heresy) through medieval scholastic refutations — Peter the Venerable's commissioned Latin [[quran]], [[thomas-aquinas]]'s *[[summa-contra-gentiles]]* — to modern apologists (Norman Geisler, [[james-white]], Nabeel Qureshi). It defends the historical [[crucifixion-of-jesus]], the textual stability of the New Testament against [[tahrif]], the high-Christological reading of Jesus's [[prostration-of-jesus|prostration]] and prayer, and the patristic identification of the [[paraclete]] as the [[holy-spirit]] rather than [[muhammad]].
