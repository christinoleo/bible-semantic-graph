The family of arguments inferring an intelligent mind from observed *specified information* — sequences carrying semiotic meaning that map non-arbitrarily onto functional outcomes. Principal contemporary defenders: [[john-lennox|John Lennox]] (*God's Undertaker*, 2009) and Stephen Meyer (*Signature in the Cell*, 2009; *Return of the God Hypothesis*, 2021), arguing that DNA's roughly 3.5 billion-letter code is functionally indistinguishable from a language — and that all known cases of specified information ultimately trace to a mind. A sub-family of the [[teleological-argument|design argument]] but distinct from the [[fine-tuning-argument|fine-tuning argument]] (which concerns physical *constants* rather than encoded *information*).
