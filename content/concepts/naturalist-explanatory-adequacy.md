The cumulative naturalist case — sharpened in Sean Carroll's *The Big Picture* (2016) and "poetic naturalism" — that the totality of well-confirmed physics, chemistry, biology, neuroscience, and cosmology coheres into a self-sufficient explanatory framework in which God is explanatorily idle. The argument deploys Ockham's Razor: granting that theism is *compatible* with the data, the simpler hypothesis (naturalism with one fewer entity) wins by parsimony. Christian apologetics responds by pointing to specific explanatory failures of naturalism — origin of the universe ([[kalam-cosmological-argument|Kalām]]), [[fine-tuning-argument|fine-tuning]], [[argument-from-consciousness|consciousness]], [[moral-argument|moral realism]] — each defended via the [[existence-of-god|five-argument cluster]] as genuine explanatory work that naturalism cannot match.
