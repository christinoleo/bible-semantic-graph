[[paul-the-apostle|Paul]]'s authentic letters were written ca. 50–60 CE, within 20–30 years of [[jesus]]'s crucifixion, and quote earlier creedal material (notably the 1 Cor 15:3-8 resurrection creed) datable to within five years of the events. [[gospel-of-mark|Mark]] is conventionally dated ca. 65–70 CE, [[gospel-of-matthew|Matthew]] and [[gospel-of-luke|Luke]] ca. 80–90 CE, and [[gospel-of-john|John]] ca. 90–110 CE — all within the lifetime of eyewitnesses or those who had heard from them. By contrast, the [[quran]]'s narrative reuse of biblical persons and events ([[mary-mother-of-jesus|Mary]], [[moses]], [[abraham]], [[jesus]], the prophets) was composed six centuries after those events and standardized decades after [[muhammad]]'s own death.
