Cyclic cosmologies — such as the Steinhardt-Turok ekpyrotic model — hypothesize an eternally repeating sequence of expansions and contractions ("Big Bangs" and "Big Crunches") with no absolute beginning. Each cycle's [[claim-second-law-thermodynamics-implies-finite-past|entropy]] is reset at the bounce, evading the heat-death objection. Refuted on the same [[claim-big-bang-cosmology-supports-finite-past|BGV grounds]] (positive average expansion rate yields a past boundary) and on cumulative-entropy grounds: even if each cycle resets, the cumulative information content over infinite cycles diverges.
