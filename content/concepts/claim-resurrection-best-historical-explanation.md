Given the [[minimal-facts-approach|minimal facts]] — execution of [[jesus]], disciples' sudden belief in his resurrection appearances, conversions of [[paul]] and [[james-brother-of-jesus|James]], empty tomb attested by women — the bodily resurrection is argued to be the best explanation by the standard historiographical criteria (explanatory scope, explanatory power, plausibility, less ad hoc, accord with accepted beliefs). Naturalistic alternatives (legend, mass hallucination, swoon, conspiracy, substitution) each fail one or more criteria; the resurrection hypothesis satisfies them jointly.
