Tu-quoque move against the Muslim rejection of Gospel authors on reliability grounds: [[hafs-ibn-sulayman|Ḥafṣ ʿan ʿĀṣim]] — the rāwī behind the dominant printed recension of the [[quran]] — was rated *matrūk al-ḥadīth* ("abandoned in ḥadīth") by classical Sunni rijāl scholars yet is fully trusted for [[quran|Qurʾān]]ic transmission, so the same standard that would discard the Gospels for authorship-criticism reasons should, applied consistently, discard the Ḥafṣ Qurʾān.
