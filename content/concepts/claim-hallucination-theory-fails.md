[[william-lane-craig|Craig]] and [[gary-habermas|Habermas]] press that the hallucination hypothesis (Lüdemann, Goulder) fails on multiple counts: clinical hallucinations are individual phenomena, not shared by groups (Paul's report of [[paul|Paul]]'s 500 simultaneous witnesses, 1 Cor 15:6, is unparalleled); hallucinations would not produce a *physical empty tomb*; and hallucinations of recently-deceased loved ones typically *do not transform the bereaved* into willing martyrs proclaiming a resurrected body. Individual subjective grief-hallucinations cannot account for the *combined* psychological, sociological, and empirical data the resurrection hypothesis explains.
