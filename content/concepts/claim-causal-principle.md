Whatever begins to exist has a cause — defenders of the [[kalam-cosmological-argument]] treat this as a metaphysically necessary truth, not a Humean empirical generalization from observed regularities. The principle is presupposed by rational inquiry itself and stands or falls with the [[principle-of-sufficient-reason]], against [[david-hume]]-style skepticism about causation.
