The Weak Anthropic Principle (Brandon Carter, 1973) observes that we *necessarily* observe a life-permitting universe, since our existence as observers presupposes it; therefore the observation of fine-tuning is unsurprising and requires no further explanation. Christian apologetics responds that the principle is tautological: it explains *why we observe* fine-tuning conditional on existence, but not *why the universe is structured* such that observers exist at all — which is the question the [[fine-tuning-argument|fine-tuning argument]] addresses.
