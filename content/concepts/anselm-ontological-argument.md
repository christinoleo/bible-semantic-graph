[[anselm-of-canterbury|Anselm]] argues in the *Proslogion* that God is "that than which nothing greater can be conceived" (*aliquid quo nihil maius cogitari possit*). If such a being existed only in the understanding, a greater being — existing also in reality — could be conceived, contradicting the definition. Therefore the greatest conceivable being must exist in reality. The argument operates wholly *a priori*, generating God's existence from the coherent concept of God alone.
