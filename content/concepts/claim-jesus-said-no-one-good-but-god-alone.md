When the rich young ruler addresses him as "good teacher," [[jesus]] replies, "Why do you call me good? No one is good but God alone" (Mark 10:18). Read in Islamic apologetic, the saying is Jesus's own explicit denial that the predicate "good" — i.e. the divine attribute — belongs to him.
