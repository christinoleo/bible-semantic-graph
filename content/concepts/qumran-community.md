The Jewish sectarian community that lived at [[qumran|Khirbet Qumran]] from c. 150 BCE to 68 CE, self-identified in the [[dead-sea-scrolls|sectarian scrolls]] as *Yaḥad* (the Community) and as the "Sons of Light" awaiting the eschatological battle against the "Sons of Darkness." Widely identified with the Essenes described by [[josephus|Josephus]], Philo, and Pliny, though some scholars dispute the identification. Led by the [[teacher-of-righteousness|Teacher of Righteousness]] against a "Wicked Priest" of the Hasmonean line, the community maintained [[zadokite|Zadokite]] priestly identity, communal property, ritual purity, and apocalyptic dualism. Destroyed by the Romans in 68 CE during the First Jewish-Roman War.
