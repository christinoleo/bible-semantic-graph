The Quranic title given to [[muhammad]] at Q 33:40, read by mainstream Sunni and Shi'a tradition as terminating the prophetic office — no prophet of any kind comes after Muhammad. The doctrine is the keystone of the [[ahmadiyya]] dispute: Ahmadis interpret *khātam* as "ornament/confirmer" rather than "final stamp," leaving room for [[mirza-ghulam-ahmad]] as a non-law-bearing prophet under Muhammad's seal — a reading the mainstream rejects, formally declaring Ahmadis non-Muslim.
