Reads Isa 9:6 — "*kî yeled yullad lānû* — for a child is born to us... and his name shall be called *pele yôʿēṣ ʾēl gibbôr ʾăbî ʿad śar šālôm* — Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God, Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace" — as predicating divine names directly of the messianic child, since *ʾēl gibbôr* ("Mighty God") is elsewhere predicated of [[yhwh]] himself (Isa 10:21). Together with [[isaiah|Isa]] 7:14's Immanuel ("God with us"), the verse is read in Christian apologetic as an Old Testament disclosure that the messianic figure shares the divine name — and therefore the divine being — with YHWH, providing prophetic ground for the [[trinity]]'s claim of plurality within the one divine essence.
