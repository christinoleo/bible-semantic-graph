Islamic eschatology holds that [[jesus|ʿĪsā ibn Maryam]] — who was not crucified (Q4:157) but raised alive to Allah (Q4:158) — will descend at the white minaret east of Damascus at the end of times, will kill [[dajjal|al-Dajjāl]] at Lod, will join the [[mahdi|Mahdī]], and per *[[sahih-al-bukhari|Bukhārī]]* 2222 will *break the cross, kill the pig, and abolish the jizya* — that is, forcibly end Christianity by removing the protected-minority status of Christians and demanding conversion. Q4:159 states that all the People of the Book "will believe in him before his death." Christian apologetics points to this profile — a "Jesus" who denies his own crucifixion, denies his deity, abolishes the gospel, and compels Islam — as a [[claim-returning-isa-of-islam-denies-deity-fits-antichrist-spirit|near-perfect match to the spirit of antichrist]] described in 1 John 4:3.
