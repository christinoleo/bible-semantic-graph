Arabic *muhaymin* (from the root *h-y-m-n*, with cognates in Aramaic / Syriac *mhayman*, "trustworthy" — the same root family as *amīn*) — applied in Q 5:48 to the [[quran]] in relation to the previous scriptures: "We have sent down the Book to you in truth, confirming the Scripture before it and *muhayminan* over it." Classical *tafsīr* splits the meaning between *guardian / protector / trustworthy witness* (the dominant medieval reading: the Qurʾān safeguards what came before) and *corrector / dominant* (the reading favoured by modern apologists pressing [[tahrif]] — the Qurʾān stands over the prior scriptures to correct them). The semantic gap is the pivot of debate: Christian apologists ([[godlogic-apologist|Avery]]) marshal the *guardian* reading to argue the Qurʾān endorses the prior scriptures' integrity; Muslim debaters ([[muhammad-hijab]]) press the *corrector* reading as licensing Qurʾānic override.
