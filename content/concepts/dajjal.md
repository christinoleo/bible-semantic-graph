In Islamic eschatology, *al-Masīḥ ad-Dajjāl* — "the false / lying messiah" — is the great deceiver who appears in the end times, claims divinity, performs apparent miracles, and dominates the earth for forty days (or forty months / forty years per varying traditions). He is one-eyed, with the word *kāfir* (disbeliever) written on his forehead, and is eventually killed by the returning [[returning-isa-islamic-eschatology|ʿĪsā]] at the Gate of Lod (Lydda) in Palestine. The primary sources are *[[sahih-muslim|Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim]]* Book 41 and *[[sahih-al-bukhari|Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī]]* Book 88. The Quran itself does not name him; he is purely hadith-derived.
