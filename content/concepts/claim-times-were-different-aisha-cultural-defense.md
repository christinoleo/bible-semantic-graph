The Islamic apologetic argument that early-7th-century Arabian marital customs — together with marital customs across the ancient world, including allegedly the marriage of [[mary-mother-of-jesus|Mary the mother of Jesus]] — permitted ages of marriage that modern societies reject, and that [[muhammad|Muhammad]]'s marriage to [[aisha|ʿĀʾisha]] should be evaluated by 7th-century cultural standards rather than 21st-century moral norms. The defense concedes the historical age and shifts the question to whether moral judgment should be culturally indexed.
