*[[sunan-al-nasai|Sunan an-Nasāʾī]]* 3378 records [[aisha|ʿĀʾisha]]'s own statement verbatim: *"The Messenger of Allah married me when I was six and consummated the marriage with me when I was nine, and I used to play with dolls."* The text is canonical, attributed to ʿĀʾisha as first-person narrator, and contained in one of the *Kutub al-Sittah* (Six Books) of authoritative Sunni hadith. It is the unambiguous source for the historical claim.
