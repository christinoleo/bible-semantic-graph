The universe is past-finite: the series of temporal events had a beginning. The claim is defended philosophically via [[claim-impossibility-of-actual-infinite]] and the impossibility of traversing an infinite by successive addition ([[al-ghazali]], [[william-lane-craig]]), and scientifically via [[big-bang-cosmology]], the second law of thermodynamics, and the Borde-Guth-Vilenkin theorem.
