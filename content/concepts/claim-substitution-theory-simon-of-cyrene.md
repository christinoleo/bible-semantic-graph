An alternative Islamic substitutionist reading identifies [[simon-of-cyrene]] — the bystander compelled to carry the cross in [[gospel-of-mark]] 15:21 — as the one "made to resemble" [[jesus]] and crucified in his place. Some tafsir traditions and Gnostic precedents (e.g., Basilides) similarly nominate Simon as the substitute on the cross.
