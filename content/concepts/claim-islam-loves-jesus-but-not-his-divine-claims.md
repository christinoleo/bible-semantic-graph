Muslims affirm [[jesus|Isa]] as a prophet and messenger of Allah, accept the Virgin Birth, call him "Messiah" and "Savior" in an eschatological sense, and believe he will return at the end of times. The disagreement with Christianity is not over Jesus's teachings but over the later Christian doctrines of [[trinity|Trinity]] and divine sonship, which Islamic apologetics attributes to post-Jesus theological development (often via [[claim-pauline-corruption-of-jesus-teaching|Pauline corruption]]) rather than to Jesus himself.
