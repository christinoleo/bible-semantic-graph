The interpretive position that the bulk of [[revelation|Revelation]] 4-22 — the seals, trumpets, bowls, the [[antichrist|Beast]], and the millennial reign — describes literal events still future to the present. Strongly associated with [[dispensationalism]] in the modern era but with antecedents in patristic premillennialism (Justin Martyr, [[irenaeus|Irenaeus]], Lactantius). Stands against [[preterism]] (events already fulfilled in 70 CE) and [[idealism]] (events symbolic of the entire church age).
