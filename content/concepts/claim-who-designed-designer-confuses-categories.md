Richard Dawkins's *God Delusion* objection that "any designer complex enough to create the universe would itself require explanation" presupposes that God is a *complex contingent being* (like a creature) rather than the simple, necessary being of classical theism whose existence does not require external explanation. As [[john-lennox|Lennox]] presses, the objection treats God as if He were just another physical object in the universe — a category error reflecting the philosophical illiteracy of the [[kalam-cosmological-argument|Kalām]]'s [[claim-cause-of-universe-is-timeless-spaceless-immaterial|conclusion]]: the cause of the universe is by definition uncaused and outside the regress, not another link in it.
