The earliest disciples — [[peter]], [[paul]], James of Zebedee, and others by patristic report — were executed or persecuted while continuing to proclaim that they had personally seen the risen [[jesus]]. The argument is sincerity, not truth: people may die for what they wrongly believe, but rarely for what they know to be fabricated. Combined with [[claim-paul-conversion-as-hostile-witness|Paul]] and [[claim-james-conversion-as-skeptical-witness|James]], this triangulates the disciples' belief that resurrection appearances had actually occurred — not whether the appearances were veridical, which is the further argumentative step.
