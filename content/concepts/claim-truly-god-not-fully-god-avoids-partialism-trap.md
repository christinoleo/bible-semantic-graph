When pressed in debate by a Muslim interlocutor on whether [[jesus]] is "fully God," the Christian apologist should answer in the wording "[[chalcedonian-christology|truly God]]" rather than "fully God" — same orthodox content (*vere Deus*), different lead-in — because *fully* invites quantitative re-interpretation toward [[partialism]] while *truly* does not.
