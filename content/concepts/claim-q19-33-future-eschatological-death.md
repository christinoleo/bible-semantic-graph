[[quran]] 19:33 — "peace is on me the day I was born and the day I will die and the day I am raised alive" — is read by classical tafsir as referring not to [[jesus]]'s first-century death but to a future natural death after his eschatological descent. This reading preserves the absolute denial of Q 4:157 while accounting for Jesus's anticipated mortality.
