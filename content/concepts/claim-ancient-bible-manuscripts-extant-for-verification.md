The Bible is supported by extant ancient manuscripts available for public scholarly verification: the Dead Sea Scrolls (Hebrew Bible witnesses 250 BCE – 70 CE), Papyrus 52 (a fragment of John ca. 125 CE), Papyri 66 and 75 (substantial portions of John and Luke ca. 200 CE), the Chester Beatty papyri, Codex Sinaiticus and Codex Vaticanus (full 4th-century Bibles), and roughly 5,800 Greek New Testament manuscripts in total. The manuscript trail is the densest of any ancient document and is openly accessible — claims about biblical corruption are testable against this record rather than asserted in the abstract.
