If [[naturalism]] and unguided [[evolution]] are jointly true, then human [[cognitive-faculties]] were selected for adaptive fitness rather than for tracking truth, so the probability that they reliably produce true beliefs is low or inscrutable — which gives a defeater for every belief they produce, including [[naturalism]] itself. The conjunction of [[naturalism]] and [[evolution]] is therefore self-defeating, whereas [[theism]] — on which God designs faculties oriented to truth — supplies exactly the [[reliabilism|reliability]] that [[naturalism]] cannot underwrite, as [[alvin-plantinga]] argues in *[[where-the-conflict-really-lies|Where the Conflict Really Lies]]*.
