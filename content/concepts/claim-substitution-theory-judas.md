The majority Sunni tafsir reading of [[quran]] 4:157's "made to resemble him to them" identifies [[judas-iscariot]] as the substitute: Allah miraculously transformed Judas to resemble [[jesus]], and the Romans crucified him instead. The plot to betray Jesus was thus turned against the betrayer himself.
