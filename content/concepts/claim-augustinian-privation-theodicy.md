[[augustine-of-hippo|Augustine]]'s classical reply to the [[claim-logical-problem-of-evil-mackie|logical problem of evil]]: evil has no positive ontological status but is the *privation of due good* (*privatio boni*), so God — as author only of being — is not the author of evil, which enters creation through the free defection of rational creatures from the good in the [[fall-of-man|Fall]]. Because evil is parasitic on goodness rather than a substance God created, the existence of evil cannot directly impeach divine goodness or omnipotence.
