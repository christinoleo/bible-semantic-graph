The four-letter mnemonic Geisler and Turek use for the historical evidence of the resurrection in *[[i-dont-have-enough-faith-to-be-an-atheist]]*: **F**atal torment — Jesus was certainly killed by Roman crucifixion (refutes [[claim-ahmadiyya-survival-thesis|swoon/survival theses]]); **E**mpty tomb — the body was missing on the third day (and [[claim-empty-tomb-women-as-first-witnesses|women were the first witnesses]] per the [[criterion-of-embarrassment|criterion of embarrassment]]); **A**ppearances — multiple, public, group, and hostile-witness appearances ([[claim-paul-conversion-as-hostile-witness|Paul]], [[claim-james-conversion-as-skeptical-witness|James]]); **T**ransformation — the disciples' radical post-crucifixion change of belief and behavior, including [[claim-disciples-died-for-belief-they-had-seen-risen-jesus|willingness to die]]. The framework runs as inference to best explanation, parallel to Habermas-Licona's [[minimal-facts-approach]] but pitched for popular use.
