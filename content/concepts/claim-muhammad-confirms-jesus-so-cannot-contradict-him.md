If the [[quran]] declares itself a [[quranic-confirmation-doctrine|confirmation of previous scripture]] (Q 3:3, 5:48) and [[muhammad]] is the prophet through whom that confirmation is delivered, then any Quranic doctrine that contradicts what [[jesus]] taught either (a) refutes the Quran's confirmation claim or (b) requires [[tahrif]] to explain the discrepancy — with the latter triggering the [[islamic-dilemma]] (a corrupted Gospel cannot also be the scripture being confirmed).
