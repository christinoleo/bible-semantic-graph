The Greek παράκλητος ("Advocate") in [[gospel-of-john]] 14-16 was originally περικλυτός ("renowned"), semantically equivalent to Arabic *Aḥmad*. The substitution masks an explicit Johannine prophecy of [[muhammad]] by name.
