The Islamic apologetic defense that [[muhammad]] is venerated as a prophet but not worshipped as divine, that he was a fallible human being who sinned as all human beings sin, and that biblical prophets — David, Moses, Abraham, Solomon — are likewise recorded as sinners. The argument frames Muhammad's contested actions as moral failures comparable to those of biblical prophets, not as disqualifying features.
