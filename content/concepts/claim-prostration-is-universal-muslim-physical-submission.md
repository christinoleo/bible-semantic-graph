*Sujūd* — forehead, nose, palms, knees, and toes touching the ground — is performed by every Muslim multiple times in each of the five daily prayers, making prostration the universal physical signature of Islamic submission to Allah. The Gospel report that [[jesus]] *fell on his face* to pray (Matt 26:39) matches this practice precisely, not the standing or seated posture of later Christian liturgy.
