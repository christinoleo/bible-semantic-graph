Classical Quranic exegesis is divided on whether *aḥmad* in [[quran]] 61:6 is a proper name; some tafsir read it as a descriptive elative ("a more praiseworthy [messenger]"). The strong identification of *Aḥmad* with [[muhammad]] is a later apologetic consolidation, not a settled premodern reading.
