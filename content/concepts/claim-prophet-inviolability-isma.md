Classical Islamic doctrine of *ʿiṣmah* holds that Allah does not abandon his prophets to humiliating death at the hands of unbelievers — to do so would impugn divine justice and the honor of [[prophet-inviolability]]. Therefore the Christian crucifixion narrative is theologically impossible regardless of what witnesses appear to say.
