The Aramaic-derived title *bar ʾenāš* — Jesus's most frequent self-designation in the Gospels (some 80 occurrences). Carries a double resonance: in Aramaic idiom it can simply mean "human being," but in [[book-of-daniel|Daniel]] 7:13-14 *"one like a son of man"* receives universal dominion from the Ancient of Days — a figure read messianically in late-Second-Temple Jewish apocalyptic (1 Enoch's *Book of Parables*; [[4-ezra|4 Ezra]] 13). [[jesus]]'s use of the title at his trial ([[claim-mark-14-62-son-of-man-at-right-hand|Mk 14:62]]) explicitly conjoins Dan 7:13 with Ps 110:1, claiming the eschatological Danielic figure as his own identity.
