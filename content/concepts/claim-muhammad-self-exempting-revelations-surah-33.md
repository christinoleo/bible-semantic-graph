Surah Al-Aḥzāb (Q33) contains a cluster of revelations specific to [[muhammad]]: Q33:50 permits him wives beyond the four-wife limit imposed on other Muslim men, including any believing woman who offers herself to him; Q33:37 authorizes his marriage to Zaynab bint Jahsh — the divorced wife of his adopted son Zayd — against existing Arab kinship taboo; Q33:53 prohibits his widows from remarrying after his death. The pattern is consistent: the revelation arrives precisely when needed to legitimize a contested marital outcome.
