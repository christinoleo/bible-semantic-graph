In John 8:58 [[jesus]] says "*prin Abraam genesthai egō eimi* — before Abraham came to be, I am," appropriating the *egō eimi* of the LXX rendering of Ex 3:14, the divine name revealed to [[moses]]. The crowd again takes up stones to kill him for blasphemy — the saying functions as a direct claim of the divine identity.
