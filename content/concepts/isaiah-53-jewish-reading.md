The Servant of [[isaiah-53]] is corporate [[israel]], identified by name as the Servant elsewhere in Deutero-Isaiah (Isa 41:8-9, 44:1-2, 49:3), suffering vicariously among the nations through exile and dispersion. The messianic-individual reading is imported retroactively from Christian polemic and ignores the unified Servant-as-Israel frame of chapters 40-55.
