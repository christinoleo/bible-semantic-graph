*[[sahih-al-bukhari|Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī]]* 3 records [[muhammad|Muhammad]]'s account of the Ḥirāʾ encounter: after being seized by an unseen being and commanded to recite, he returned home trembling, fearing for his life and (per sirah parallels) considering throwing himself off a mountain. It was [[khadija]] who reassured him the experience was not demonic, then took him to her Christian cousin [[waraqa-ibn-nawfal|Waraqa ibn Nawfal]], who identified the being as the Nāmūs that had come to Moses. The prophet's own first reading was demonic; the angelic interpretation came secondhand through a comforting wife and a Christian cousin — a chain of *external* identification rather than internal divine confirmation.
