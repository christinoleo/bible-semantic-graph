Josephus, *Antiquities* 18.63-64 (the *Testimonium Flavianum*), even after critically stripping the disputed Christian interpolations, retains a minimal core confirming that [[jesus]] was condemned by [[pontius-pilate]] to the cross. This is an independent first-century Jewish witness outside the Christian textual tradition.
