[[stephen-wykstra]], [[william-alston]], and [[michael-bergmann]]: from "I cannot identify a good that would justify this evil" it does not follow that "no such good exists," because human cognitive access to the divine moral economy is too limited to license evidential inferences in either direction. The position defeats Rowe-style probabilistic arguments without committing to any positive theodicy.
