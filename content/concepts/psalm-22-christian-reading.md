[[psalm-22]] prefigures [[jesus]]'s crucifixion with striking specificity — "they pierced my hands and feet" (22:16 LXX), "they divide my garments among them and cast lots" (22:18) — and Jesus himself cites its opening cry from the cross ([[gospel-of-mark]] 15:34). All four evangelists weave Psalm 22 into the passion narrative as deliberate fulfillment.
