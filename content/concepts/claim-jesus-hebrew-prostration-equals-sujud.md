The Hebrew *hishtaḥawah* (Gen 17:3, [[abraham]] falls on his face) and the Aramaic/Syriac cognates share a Common-Semitic root and functional field with Arabic *sujūd* — bodily prostration as submission to the one God. The Gospel scene of [[jesus]] falling on his face (Matt 26:39) therefore inherits the same Semitic posture-vocabulary that Islam codifies as the central act of *ṣalāh*.
