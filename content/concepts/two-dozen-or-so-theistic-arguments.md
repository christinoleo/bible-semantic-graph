Informal catalog of theistic arguments compiled by [[alvin-plantinga|Plantinga]] in a 1986 lecture (published 2007), sketching some 26 distinct arguments grouped by inferential structure: half-a-dozen ontological arguments, cosmological arguments, teleological arguments (including [[fine-tuning-argument|fine-tuning]]), arguments from intentionality, arguments from consciousness, the [[claim-plantinga-eaan|argument from proper function]] (later EAAN), moral arguments, arguments from love, beauty, play, and arguments from religious experience. Plantinga's point is not that any one is decisive but that the *cumulative case* matters and that natural theology is a far richer field than the standard textbook five.
