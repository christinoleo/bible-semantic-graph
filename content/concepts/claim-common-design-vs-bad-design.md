Apparent biological suboptimality cited by the [[argument-from-poor-design|poor-design argument]] is equally consistent with a single designer reusing shared patterns across organisms — the [[teleological-argument|common-design hypothesis]] — much as a software engineer reuses libraries subject to engineering tradeoffs. Because the same anatomical data underdetermines the choice between "no designer" and "designer working with constraints," [[claim-suboptimal-biology-refutes-design|the suboptimality inference]] fails to discriminate against the [[existence-of-god|theistic hypothesis]].
