The interpretive position that [[revelation|Revelation]] depicts symbolically the ongoing spiritual conflict between Christ and the powers of evil throughout the *entire church age*, without anchoring its imagery to specific past or future events. Influential in Augustinian, [[amillennialism|amillennial]], and modern Reformed readings (William Hendriksen, Anthony Hoekema, G.K. Beale).
