J.L. Mackie's 1955 formulation: the propositions (1) God is omnipotent, (2) God is omniscient, (3) God is morally perfect, and (4) evil exists are jointly logically inconsistent. An omnipotent being could prevent any evil; an omniscient being would know how; a morally perfect being would want to. The set is therefore a strict contradiction, and the existence of any evil whatsoever logically refutes the existence of the theistic God. Widely regarded as decisively answered by [[plantinga-free-will-defense|Plantinga's Free Will Defense]] (1974) — even atheist philosophers (William Rowe, Paul Draper) now grant that the *logical* PoE has failed and have shifted to the *evidential* form.
