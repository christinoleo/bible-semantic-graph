The meta-hub for the atheist / naturalist case organized in this graph as a structural parallel to the [[christian-apologetics-corpus|Christian apologetics corpus]]. Five clusters of positive arguments *for* the non-existence of God or *for* the explanatory adequacy of naturalism: the [[problem-of-evil|Problem of Evil]] (the historically strongest objection), the [[divine-hiddenness-argument|Argument from Divine Hiddenness]] (Schellenberg), [[naturalist-explanatory-adequacy|Naturalist Explanatory Adequacy]] (Sean Carroll, Sean Carroll-style "Big Picture" naturalism), the [[cognitive-science-of-religion|Cognitive Science of Religion]] (debunking the *origin* of theistic belief), and the [[argument-from-poor-design|Argument from Poor Design]] (biological suboptimality undermining design inference). The [[existence-of-god|positive theistic case]] and this corpus are the two halves of the *natural-theology / natural-atheology* dialectic.
