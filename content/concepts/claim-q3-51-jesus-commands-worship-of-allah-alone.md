Quran 3:51 places on [[jesus]]'s lips: "*Inna llāha rabbī wa-rabbukum fa-ʿbudūhu — hādhā ṣirāṭun mustaqīm*" — "Indeed Allah is my Lord and your Lord, so worship Him; this is the straight path." The Quranic Jesus locates himself among the worshippers, not as object of worship.
