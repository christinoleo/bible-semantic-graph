A clergyman who abuses children acts *in defiance of* the explicit teaching of [[jesus]] (Mt 18:6, Mk 9:42, Mt 19:14) and is condemned by Christian doctrine itself. The Christian apologetic critique of [[muhammad]], by contrast, derives from Islam's *foundational* sources — the Quran, Sahih hadith, and Sirah — depicting the prophet's own conduct, not the conduct of later followers in violation of doctrine. The two categories are not parallel: bad followers indict followers; foundational texts indict the foundation.
