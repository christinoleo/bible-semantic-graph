The cluster of statements in the canonical Gospels in which [[jesus]] speaks about his own identity, mission, and authority — claims to be the [[gospel-of-john|Son of God]] (Jn 3:16, 6:38-40), the [[claim-mark-14-62-son-of-man-at-right-hand|Son of Man at the right hand]], the final judge of mankind (Jn 5:22-29), and the savior whose voice the dead will hear. Christian readings take these as the doctrinal foundation of Christology; Islamic readings, which present Jesus as a prophet of Allah, must either treat these passages as [[tahrif|corrupted]] or as misrepresented by [[claim-pauline-corruption-of-jesus-teaching|Pauline interpolation]].
