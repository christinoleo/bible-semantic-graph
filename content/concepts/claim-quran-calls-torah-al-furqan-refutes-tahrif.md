The [[quran]] designates the [[torah]] given to [[moses]] as *al-[[furqan|Furqān]]* — the *criterion* by which truth is distinguished from falsehood (Q 21:48, Q 2:53). The predication is incompatible with strong [[tahrif]]: a textually corrupted Torah cannot intelligibly serve as a Qurʾānically endorsed criterion of truth. The argument runs strictly inside Qurʾānic terms — like [[claim-quran-commands-following-extant-injil|the Q 5:47 case]], it refutes pre-Muhammad textual corruption without appealing to Christian premises.
