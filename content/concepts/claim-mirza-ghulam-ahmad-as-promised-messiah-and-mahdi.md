[[mirza-ghulam-ahmad]] claimed in 1889 to be both the returned [[jesus|Masīḥ]] of Islamic eschatology and the promised [[muhammad-al-mahdi|Mahdi]], and additionally a non-law-bearing prophet under [[muhammad]]'s seal. The claim is doctrinally possible only on (i) the [[claim-ahmadiyya-survival-thesis|survival reading]] of the crucifixion (Jesus must already be dead naturally to vacate the office), and (ii) a non-finality reading of [[khatam-an-nabiyyin|khātam an-nabiyyīn]] at Q 33:40. Mainstream Sunni and Shi'a authorities reject both moves and accordingly declare Ahmadis non-Muslim (Pakistan, Second Amendment, 1974).
