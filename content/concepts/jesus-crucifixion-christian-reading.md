The crucifixion of [[jesus]] is overdetermined by independent ancient sources (four Gospels, pre-Pauline creed in [[1-corinthians]] 15:3-8, Tacitus, Josephus) and accepted by mainstream critical-historical scholarship across faith commitments. The Islamic denial of Q 4:157 requires [[tahrif]] at a scale the manuscript record does not support.
