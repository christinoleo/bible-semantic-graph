The prophetic theme of YHWH's decisive future intervention — bringing judgment on his enemies (and frequently on his own unfaithful people) and inaugurating his reign — running through the prophetic literature (Amos 5:18-20; Isa 13; Joel 1-3; Obad 15; Zeph 1) and central to the [[twelve-minor-prophets|Book of the Twelve]]. In the [[new-testament|New Testament]] the same theme is transposed onto the [[second-coming-of-christ|Day of the Lord Jesus]] ([[pauline-epistles|1 Cor]] 1:8; 5:5; 1 Thess 5:2; 2 Pet 3:10), inheriting both the judgment and restoration dimensions of the prophetic theme.
