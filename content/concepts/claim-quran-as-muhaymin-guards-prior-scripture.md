Q 5:48 calls the [[quran]] *muhaymin* over the previous scriptures. Read in the classical sense — *guardian / trustworthy witness* (matching the Aramaic-Syriac cognate *mhayman*, "trustworthy") — the verse precludes [[tahrif]]: a guardian protects an integral text, it does not authorize corrupting it. Muslim apologists who push the alternative reading (*muhaymin = corrector*) must defend that semantic against the classical *tafsīr* tradition and against the [[muhaymin|Q 59:23 use of al-Muhaymin]] as a divine name, where the corrector-reading would be incongruous.
