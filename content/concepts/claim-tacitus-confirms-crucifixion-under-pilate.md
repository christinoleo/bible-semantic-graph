Tacitus, *Annales* 15.44, writes that "Christus suffered the extreme penalty during the reign of Tiberius at the hands of one of our procurators, [[pontius-pilate]]" — a hostile, non-Christian Roman source confirming the bare historical fact of [[jesus]]'s execution. The witness is independent of Christian transmission and predates any plausible window for [[tahrif]] of the Roman archive.
