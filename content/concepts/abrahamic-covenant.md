The covenant established by [[yhwh]] with [[abraham]], comprising three core elements: **land** (the promise of [[canaan]]), **descendants** (the promise of innumerable offspring), and **blessing** (the promise that "all families of the earth shall be blessed in you").

The covenant is reaffirmed at multiple narrative points (Gen 12, 15, 17, 22) and frames the entire patriarchal cycle. It is taken up and reread by later traditions — Mosaic ([[yhwh]] as the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob), Davidic (the promise to David as covenantal fulfillment), and, in Christian interpretation, as fulfilled in the Church.

The relationship between the Abrahamic Covenant and the [[sacrifice-of-isaac]] is debated: some read the Akedah as a covenantal renewal under the most extreme conditions; others as a deliberate disruption of the covenant's expected continuity.
