The comparative debate over which scripture — the Bible or the Quran — has been more faithfully preserved across time. Islamic apologetics typically points to memorization traditions (*ḥifẓ*) and the Uthmanic standardization as evidence the Quran is uniquely uncorrupted, while citing English Bible-version diversity (KJV vs NIV) as evidence of Christian corruption. Christian apologetics distinguishes English translation from underlying manuscript transmission, points to extensive [[claim-qira-at-arabic-textual-variants|Quranic Qira'at variants]], and presses the [[islamic-dilemma|deeper dilemma]] that Islam's scripture validates the very texts it must also accuse of corruption.
