At the Jordan all three persons appear in one scene: the Son ([[jesus]]) ascends from the water, the Spirit of God descends "*hōsei peristeran* — like a dove," and the Father's voice declares "*houtos estin ho huios mou ho agapētos* — this is my beloved Son" (echoing [[psalms|Ps]] 2:7 and Isa 42:1). The simultaneity of three distinct manifestations under one act of divine self-revelation is read as a narrative disclosure of the [[trinity]].
