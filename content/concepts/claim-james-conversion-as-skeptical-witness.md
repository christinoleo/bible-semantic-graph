The Gospel tradition records that Jesus's biological brothers — including [[james-brother-of-jesus|James]] — did not believe in him during his ministry (Mark 3:21; John 7:5). After the crucifixion James is suddenly a leader of the Jerusalem church (Gal 1:19, Acts 15) and dies a martyr's death under the high priest Ananus (Josephus, *Antiquities* 20.9.1). 1 Cor 15:7 attributes the reversal to a resurrection appearance — a private explanation that the New Testament otherwise has little narrative motive to invent.
