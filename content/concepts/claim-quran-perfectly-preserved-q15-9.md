The cornerstone Islamic apologetic that the [[quran|Qurʾān]] has been preserved verbatim and uniform since its revelation — anchored in Q 15:9 ("Indeed, We sent down the *dhikr* and indeed We are its guardian") and the global *ḥifẓ* tradition. The claim is the structural counterweight to [[tahrif]]: it asymmetrically privileges the Qurʾān against [[bible|the Bible]] in [[bible-vs-quran-preservation]], and is the implicit premise of standard Deedat-Naik dawah polemic. Its weakest joint is the documented diversity of the [[claim-qira-at-arabic-textual-variants|canonical qirāʾāt]], the [[claim-uthmanic-burning-of-quran-variants|Uthmanic burning of competing codices]], and the [[claim-hadith-of-forgotten-and-lost-quranic-verses|ḥadīth memory of lost verses]].
