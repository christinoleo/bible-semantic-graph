The position that the thousand-year reign of [[revelation|Revelation]] 20 is symbolic of the *entire church age* between Christ's first and [[second-coming-of-christ|second comings]] — there is no literal future millennium on earth. Articulated by [[augustine-of-hippo|Augustine]] in *City of God* and dominant in medieval and Reformation churches; the default position of modern Reformed, Lutheran, and Roman Catholic theology.
