The Islamic apologetic reading identifies the [[antichrist|biblical Antichrist]] (1 Jn 2:18, [[revelation|Rev]] 13) with [[dajjal|al-Dajjāl]] of Islamic eschatology: both are end-times deceivers claiming false divinity, both perform false signs, both dominate the earth for a limited time, both are defeated by Christ — who in both schemes returns to kill the false messiah. On this reading, Islam and Christianity *share* the same villain and share the same Christ-as-defeater, with Islam simply correcting later Christian doctrines about Jesus's nature. The reading is rebutted by [[claim-returning-isa-of-islam-denies-deity-fits-antichrist-spirit|the Johannine criterion]]: the *returning Christ* of the two systems is identified differently — and a returning Christ who denies his deity matches the antichrist, not the Christ.
