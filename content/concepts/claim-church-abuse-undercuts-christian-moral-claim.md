The argument that the documented record of clergy sexual abuse and institutional cover-up in Catholic and Protestant churches disqualifies Christians from making moral judgments about [[muhammad]]'s conduct. The argument is a *tu quoque*: it does not directly defend the historical record of Muhammad's actions but neutralizes the rhetorical standing of the accuser. Distinct from the [[claim-bad-followers-distinct-from-bad-foundations|bad-followers-vs-bad-foundations]] reply that this argument invites.
