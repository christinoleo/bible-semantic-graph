The Paraclete of [[gospel-of-john]] 14-16 is the Holy Spirit, fulfilled at Pentecost ([[acts]] 2). No manuscript witnesses περικλυτός; παράκλητος and περικλυτός are not visually or morphologically near-equivalent.
