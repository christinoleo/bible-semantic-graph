The First [[council-of-nicaea]] (325 CE) deliberated the [[arianism|Arian controversy]] and defined the Christological homoousios formula — it did not produce, ratify, or vote on the New Testament canon. The list of the 27 NT books in the form Christians now use first appears in [[athanasius|Athanasius]]'s 39th Festal Letter (367 CE), forty-two years later, and reflects long-standing usage rather than conciliar decree. The popular claim, traceable to Voltaire and widely re-popularized by Dan Brown's *The Da Vinci Code* (2003), is one of the standard misconceptions [[wesley-huff]] addresses in his public work.
