Capital established by [[david]] after capturing the Jebusite stronghold, site of the First Temple built by Solomon on [[moriah]], destroyed by Babylon in 586 BCE, and rebuilt as the Second Temple after the return from exile. The crucifixion of [[jesus]] takes place just outside its walls, and [[acts]] places the descent of the Spirit and the earliest [[jesus-movement]] community here.

Destroyed again by the Romans under Titus in 70 CE following the First Jewish Revolt — an event that ends the sacrificial cult and reshapes both [[rabbinic-judaism]] and emergent Christianity. Refounded by Hadrian as Aelia Capitolina after 135 CE. The city is sacred to Judaism (Temple Mount, Western Wall), Christianity (Holy Sepulchre, Mount of Olives), and Islam (al-Aqsa, Dome of the Rock).
