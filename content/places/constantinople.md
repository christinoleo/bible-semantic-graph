Founded by Constantine in 330 CE on the site of the older Greek colony of Byzantium and dedicated as "New Rome." Capital of the [[byzantine-empire]] for over a millennium until its fall to the Ottomans in 1453, after which it became Istanbul. Seat of the [[ecumenical-patriarchate]] and one of the five ancient patriarchates of the early church alongside [[rome]], [[alexandria]], [[antioch]], and [[jerusalem]].

Host city of the First (381) and Second (553) Councils of Constantinople, where the Nicene Creed received its developed pneumatological form and the Christological settlement of [[chalcedon]] was extended. The city's rivalry with [[rome]] for ecclesial primacy culminated in the [[great-schism]] of 1054.
