Ur is the city from which [[abraham]] (then Abram) departs at the call of [[yhwh]]. Identified by most scholars with the Sumerian city of Ur in southern Mesopotamia (modern Tell el-Muqayyar, Iraq), though some have proposed a northern Ur near Harran.

The cultural-religious break implied by leaving Ur — a major center of moon-god (Nanna/Sin) worship — frames the [[abrahamic-covenant]] as a movement from polytheism toward exclusive YHWH-worship, a framing that is more theological than historical.
