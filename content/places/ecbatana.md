Capital of ancient Media in the Zagros Mountains (modern Hamadan, Iran), summer residence of the Achaemenid Persian kings, mentioned in [[ezra|Ezra]] 6:2 (Achmetha) as the location where the decree of Cyrus authorizing the rebuilding of the [[temple|Jerusalem Temple]] was found in the royal archives. Setting for portions of the book of [[book-of-tobit|Tobit]] (the city of Raguel and Sarah). Conquered by [[alexander-the-great|Alexander the Great]] in 330 BCE.
