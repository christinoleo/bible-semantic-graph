Small town in the hill country of Judah, roughly ten kilometers south of [[jerusalem]]. Hometown of [[david]] in [[1-samuel]] 16 and the setting of [[book-of-ruth]]. Micah 5:2 names it as the foretold origin of the future Davidic ruler, a prophecy [[gospel-of-matthew]] and [[gospel-of-luke]] both invoke when narrating the birth of [[jesus]] here.

The Hebrew name means "house of bread"; the Arabic Bayt Laḥm preserves the same root. The Church of the Nativity, built over the traditional cave of the birth, has been a major Christian pilgrimage site since the fourth century.
