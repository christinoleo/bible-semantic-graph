Ancient Median city southeast of Tehran (modern Shahr-e Rey, Iran), prominent in the book of [[book-of-tobit|Tobit]] as the city where Tobit deposited ten talents of silver with Gabael — the recovery of which provides the narrative impetus for Tobiah's journey under the guidance of [[raphael-archangel|Raphael]]. Mentioned in [[1-maccabees|1 Maccabees]] 6:56 as the eastern garrison of the Seleucid empire. Briefly the Parthian capital before Ctesiphon; destroyed by the Mongols in 1220.
