Capital of the Neo-[[assyria|Assyrian]] Empire from the reign of Sennacherib (c. 700 BCE) until its destruction by the Medo-Babylonian alliance in 612 BCE. The setting of the prophetic mission of [[jonah]] ([[book-of-jonah|Book of Jonah]]), the great power against which [[book-of-nahum|Nahum]] prophesies, and the seat from which Sennacherib threatened [[jerusalem|Jerusalem]] in [[hezekiah|Hezekiah]]'s reign (2 Kgs 18-19; [[book-of-isaiah|Isa]] 36-37; 2 Chr 32). Its ruins lie opposite modern Mosul in northern Iraq.
