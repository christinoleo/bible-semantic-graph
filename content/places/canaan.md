Canaan is the land promised to [[abraham]] and his descendants in the [[abrahamic-covenant]]. Geographically spans roughly the Levant — modern Israel, Palestine, Lebanon, and parts of Syria and Jordan. Inhabited at the time of the patriarchal narratives by a mosaic of peoples (Canaanites, Amorites, Hittites, Jebusites, et al.), whose religious practices form much of the polemical backdrop of the Hebrew Bible.
