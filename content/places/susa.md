Ancient city in southwestern Iran, capital of the Elamite kingdom and later one of the four imperial capitals of the Achaemenid Persian Empire alongside Persepolis, Ecbatana, and Babylon. The Apadana palace built by Darius I made it the principal administrative seat of Persian kings from the late 6th through 4th centuries BCE.

Primary setting of the book of [[esther]], where the Jewish queen and Mordecai navigate court intrigue under Ahasuerus (Xerxes I). [[daniel]] receives the vision of the ram and the goat at the Ulai canal of Susa in [[book-of-daniel]] 8, and [[nehemiah]] is cupbearer to Artaxerxes here when he learns of [[jerusalem]]'s ruined walls. The city is thus the diaspora setting where post-exilic Judaism negotiates life under a tolerant but capricious imperial power.
