The land along the eastern Mediterranean coast promised by YHWH to [[abraham|Abraham]] (Gen 12:1-7, 15:18-21) and his descendants, occupied under [[joshua]] (Joshua 1-12), unified under [[david|David]] and [[solomon|Solomon]], divided into the northern kingdom of Israel and the southern kingdom of Judah after Solomon, and reduced to Judah alone after the Assyrian conquest of 722 BCE. Center of biblical narrative; site of the [[temple|Temple]] in [[jerusalem|Jerusalem]]; central to Jewish messianic expectation and to all three Abrahamic eschatologies.
