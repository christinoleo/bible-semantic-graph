Mesopotamian empire centered on the upper Tigris valley, dominant in the Ancient Near East from the 9th to the 7th centuries BCE under kings including Tiglath-Pileser III, Shalmaneser V, Sargon II, Sennacherib, and Esarhaddon. Conquered the northern kingdom of Israel in 722 BCE — exiling the ten tribes whose subsequent disappearance generated the "lost tribes" tradition — and besieged [[jerusalem|Judah]] under [[hezekiah|Hezekiah]] (701 BCE). Capitals included Aššur, Kalhu, and finally [[nineveh|Nineveh]]. Fell to the Medo-Babylonian alliance in 612–609 BCE.
