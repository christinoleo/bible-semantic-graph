The mountain to which [[abraham]] travels for the [[sacrifice-of-isaac]]. 2 Chronicles 3:1 identifies it with the site of Solomon's Temple in Jerusalem — a later editorial linkage that establishes the Temple Mount as the geographic center of [[yhwh]]'s covenantal action.
