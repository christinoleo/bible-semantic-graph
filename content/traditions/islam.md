The Abrahamic monotheistic tradition founded by [[muhammad|Muhammad]] in seventh-century Arabia, with the [[quran|Qurʾān]] as primary scripture, the *sunna* (preserved in [[sahih-al-bukhari|Bukhārī]], [[sahih-muslim|Muslim]], and other hadith collections) as secondary normative tradition, and *sharīʿa* derived from both as legal-ethical framework. Divided primarily between Sunnī (c. 85%) and Shīʿī (c. 15%) communions, with secondary divisions including Sufi mystical orders, [[ahmadiyya|Aḥmadiyya]], and modern reformist movements. The world's second-largest religion (c. 1.9 billion adherents).
