Restorationist Christian movement founded by Charles Taze Russell in the United States in the late nineteenth century, organized institutionally by Joseph Franklin Rutherford after Russell's death and centralized in the Watch Tower Bible and Tract Society. Distinguished by anti-Trinitarian Christology ([[jesus]] is the created archangel [[michael-archangel|Michael]]), rejection of the immortality of the soul, refusal of military service and blood transfusions, and a long history of failed end-times predictions (1914, 1918, 1925, 1975). Maintains its own translation, the *New World Translation*, whose Christology has been heavily criticized by mainstream biblical scholarship.
