The argumentative and doctrinal tradition of the Church Fathers (c. 2nd–8th centuries), spanning Greek (Athanasius, the Cappadocians, John Chrysostom, John of Damascus), Latin (Tertullian, Ambrose, Augustine, Jerome), and Syriac (Ephrem) lineages. Forged the Nicene–Chalcedonian creedal grammar that downstream [[christian-apologetic]] and [[scholastic]] reasoning takes for granted.

Method is heavily exegetical and typological, deferring to apostolic tradition and conciliar definitions ([[council-of-nicaea]], [[council-of-chalcedon]]). See `/axis/tradition/patristic` for Arguments grounded in this tradition.
