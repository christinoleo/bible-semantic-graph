Theological system originating with John Nelson Darby (1800-1882) and popularized in the United States through the *Scofield Reference Bible* (1909) which divides salvation history into distinct *dispensations*, sharply distinguishes Israel from the Church as parallel-but-separate peoples of God, and reads end-times prophecy [[futurism|futuristically]] — typically with a pre-tribulation rapture, a seven-year tribulation, the [[antichrist|antichrist]]ic Beast, and a visible [[second-coming-of-christ|return of Christ]] to establish a literal thousand-year reign. The dominant lay-evangelical eschatology in 20th- and 21st-century America; the interpretive home of [[tim-lahaye|LaHaye]], [[hal-lindsey|Lindsey]], and (with the [[claim-islamic-mahdi-fits-biblical-antichrist-profile|Islamic-Antichrist twist]]) [[joel-richardson|Joel Richardson]].
