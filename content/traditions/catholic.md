The Roman Catholic Church — the largest single Christian communion (c. 1.3 billion adherents), holding doctrinal continuity from the apostolic age through the historic patriarchate of Rome, the development of the papacy as universal pastor, and the dogmatic teaching of the ecumenical councils up through Vatican II (1962-1965). Distinguished from [[reformed|Reformed]] and other Protestant traditions by the sacramental system (seven sacraments), Marian dogmas, the magisterium, and the priesthood. The interpretive home of [[thomas-aquinas|Aquinas]], the Scholastic synthesis, and the modern *Catechism* (1992).
