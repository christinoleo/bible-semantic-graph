Eschatological battle plan from Cave 1, [[qumran]], 1947. Hebrew, ~50-1 BCE on paleography. Nineteen columns extant; opening and conclusion lost. Fragments from Cave 4 (4Q491-496) preserve recensional variants and complicate the relationship between recensions.

Programs a 40-year apocalyptic war between the "Sons of Light" — the [[qumran-community]], the tribes of Levi, Judah, Benjamin, returning exiles — and the "Sons of Darkness" — the [[kittim]] (likely Romans, possibly Seleucids in earlier strata) plus their allies (Edom, Moab, Ammon, Philistia, Amalek). Detailed: trumpet inscriptions, banner texts, troop formations, priestly prayers, the order of battle for each year of the seven-decisive engagements, the climactic intervention of [[michael-archangel]] and the heavenly host.

Genre mixes [[holy-war]] traditions from [[deuteronomy]] and [[book-of-joshua]] with Hellenistic military manuals (column 9 echoes Greek/Roman tactical treatises). Heavily indebted to [[book-of-daniel]] chapters 11-12 for its periodization and angelology. Witnesses to the militant strand of Second-Temple apocalyptic that also shaped [[revelation]] and the [[bar-kokhba-revolt]] imagination.

Not a textual witness to any canonical book, but a primary source for the eschatology that the [[new-testament]] writers inherited and modified. Shrine of the Book, Israel Museum.
