Black basalt monumental inscription erected by King Mesha of Moab (c. 840 BCE) and discovered at Dhiban (Dibon, Jordan) in 1868. The principal extra-biblical witness to ninth-century Levantine history: Mesha narrates his rebellion against the Omride dynasty of Israel (paralleling [[2-kings|2 Kgs]] 3) and credits his god Chemosh with victory. Contains the earliest known extra-biblical references to YHWH (line 18) and possibly to the "House of David" (a contested reading of line 31). Now in the Louvre.
