Foundational rule-text of the [[qumran]] sectarian community, recovered from Cave 1 in 1947. Hebrew, ~100-75 BCE on paleography. Eleven columns; a near-complete copy. Additional fragments (4QS^a-j, 5QS) from Caves 4 and 5 show that the rule circulated in multiple recensions — 4QS variants sometimes shorter than 1QS, complicating any "original" reconstruction.

Lays out admission procedures, communal discipline, calendar regulations, hierarchies (the Many, the priests, the [[teacher-of-righteousness]] tradition), oaths, and penal code for the [[yahad]] community. Theologically marked by the [[two-spirits-doctrine]] in columns 3-4 — a strict dualism of light vs darkness, truth vs falsehood, the Prince of Light vs the Angel of Darkness — that has shaped scholarly discussion of Johannine and Pauline dualism since the scroll's publication.

Not a biblical witness in the textual-critical sense — it does not preserve a canonical book — but indispensable for understanding the religious world that produced the biblical scrolls at Qumran. Identification of the community with the [[essenes]] (per Pliny, Philo, Josephus) is the majority view but contested; alternatives include a broader proto-sectarian Judean movement.

Held at the Shrine of the Book. Standard critical edition: Charlesworth (PTSDSSP). Cf. [[damascus-document]] for the related but distinct legal rule-tradition.
