The narrative recorded in [[genesis|Genesis]] 3 of [[adam|Adam]] and Eve's disobedience to the divine command not to eat from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, prompted by the serpent's deception, resulting in expulsion from Eden, the introduction of toil, pain, and death into human existence, and — in subsequent Christian theology — the inheritable corruption of human nature ([[original-sin|original sin]]). Variously read as historical event (traditional), as theological-mythological reflection on the universal human condition (modernist), or as Ancient Near Eastern etiological narrative analogous to other ANE expulsion myths (critical scholarship).
