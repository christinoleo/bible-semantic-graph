David Wood (b. 1976) — American Christian apologist with a PhD in philosophy from Fordham, founder of the Acts 17 Apologetics media platform, and the friend whose multi-year challenge to [[nabeel-qureshi]] during their time as roommates at Old Dominion University led directly to Qureshi's conversion (narrated in *[[seeking-allah-finding-jesus]]*).

Specializes in the historical and textual critique of Islam — the reliability of hadith, [[muhammad]]'s biography from Muslim primary sources, the [[quran]]'s composition history. His debating circuit overlaps heavily with [[zakir-naik]] and the post-[[ahmed-deedat]] generation of Muslim apologists.
