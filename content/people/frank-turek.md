Frank Turek (b. 1961) — American Christian apologist, founder of CrossExamined.org Ministries, and the popularizing voice behind the twelve-point framework he co-developed with his teacher [[norman-geisler]] in *[[i-dont-have-enough-faith-to-be-an-atheist]]* (2004). Career arc: U.S. Navy officer → corporate consulting → full-time apologetics from the early 2000s; PhD under Geisler at Southern Evangelical Seminary.

His 2014 *Stealing from God* extends the moral and epistemological arguments of the 2004 book — the move that secular ethics, science, and reason cannot consistently account for their own preconditions without borrowing from theism. The CrossExamined campus-debate and online video circuit makes him one of the most-watched contemporary Christian apologists in the Anglosphere.
