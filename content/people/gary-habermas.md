Gary Habermas (b. 1950) — American philosopher and historian at Liberty University whose career has been almost entirely organized around the historical defense of the [[resurrection-of-jesus]]. The architect of the [[minimal-facts-approach]] — the apologetic move of building the resurrection argument only on data accepted by the broad consensus of critical scholarship (including skeptics like [[bart-ehrman]] on most points), so that the conclusion does not depend on prior commitment to biblical inerrancy.

Doctoral supervisor of [[nabeel-qureshi]] and the proximate source for the historical framework Qureshi deploys in *[[no-god-but-one]]*. The 2004 *[[the-case-for-the-resurrection-of-jesus]]* (with [[michael-licona]]) is the standard reference for the framework.
