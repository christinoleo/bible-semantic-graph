Bedouin wet-nurse of the Banū Saʿd ibn Bakr who fostered [[muhammad|Muhammad]] in his early childhood (c. 570-574 CE), following the Quraysh custom of placing infants of noble families with desert tribes for physical hardihood and pure Arabic. According to Ibn Hisham's *Sīrah*, after the [[shaqq-al-sadr|chest-splitting]] incident she and her husband Ḥārith returned Muhammad to his mother Āmina earlier than the normal fostering period would have ended — a detail Christian apologetics treats as a [[claim-shaqq-al-sadr-halima-returned-muhammad-frightened|sirah-internal admission of disturbance]].
