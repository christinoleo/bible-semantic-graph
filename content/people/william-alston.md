American philosopher of religion (1921–2009), one of the principal architects (with [[alvin-plantinga|Alvin Plantinga]] and Nicholas Wolterstorff) of Reformed Epistemology — the position that belief in God can be epistemically rational without inferential grounding. Alston's specific contribution was the analysis of "Christian Mystical Practice" as a *doxastic practice* analogous to sense-perception (*Perceiving God*, 1991), arguing for the rationality of trusting religious experience as a source of belief about God when properly socially-and-tradition-mediated.
