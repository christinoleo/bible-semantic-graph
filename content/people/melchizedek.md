Priest-king of Salem (identified with [[jerusalem|Jerusalem]]) who blessed [[abraham|Abraham]] and received a tenth from him after the rescue of [[lot]] ([[genesis|Gen]] 14:18-20). His name means "King of Righteousness." [[psalms|Psalm]] 110:4 declares the Davidic king "a priest forever after the order of Melchizedek." [[hebrews|Hebrews]] 5-7 develops the typology at length, arguing that the priesthood of [[jesus]] is superior to the levitical priesthood because it is patterned on Melchizedek — a priest "without genealogy" who appears suddenly and prefigures Christ's eternal high priesthood.
