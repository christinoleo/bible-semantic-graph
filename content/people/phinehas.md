Grandson of [[aaron]] and son of Eleazar the high priest, whose decisive action at Baal-Peor — running a spear through Zimri and the Midianite woman Cozbi during the apostasy with the Midianite cult ([[numbers|Num]] 25:6-15) — halted a plague and earned him and his line a "covenant of perpetual priesthood" and a "covenant of peace" from YHWH (Num 25:11-13; cf. Ps 106:30-31, where his act is "reckoned to him as righteousness" — directly paralleling Gen 15:6 on [[abraham|Abraham]]). The locus classicus of "righteous zeal" in Second-Temple Judaism (1 Macc 2:26, 54; Sir 45:23-24) and a complex moral exemplum in subsequent Jewish ethical reflection.
