Sephardic Jewish philosopher, halakhic codifier, and physician (1138–1204), born in Córdoba, fled Almohad persecution to Fez and ultimately to Fustat (Egypt) where he served as court physician to Saladin's vizier. Author of the *Mishneh Torah* (a comprehensive code of Jewish law in fourteen books) and the *Guide for the Perplexed* (a philosophical reconciliation of [[torah|Torah]] with Aristotelian metaphysics). His "Thirteen Principles of Faith" remain the most-recited Jewish creedal formulation. The single most influential post-Talmudic Jewish thinker.
