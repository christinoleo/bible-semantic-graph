Melkite (Greek Orthodox in communion with Rome's pre-1054 see-of-Antioch lineage) bishop of Sidon in the twelfth century, author of the *Letter to a Muslim Friend* — an Arabic-language Christian apologetic addressing Islamic objections to Christian doctrine while granting the divine origin of the [[quran|Qurʾān]] as a particular-audience revelation. The letter circulated widely, prompted Muslim responses by [[ibn-taymiyya|Ibn Taymiyya]] (*al-Jawāb al-Ṣaḥīḥ*) and al-Qarafi (*al-Ajwiba al-Fākhira*) — making Paul a key node in pre-modern Christian-Islamic intellectual exchange.
