Roman general and statesman (106–48 BCE), conqueror of the eastern Mediterranean and member of the First Triumvirate with Caesar and Crassus. In 63 BCE he intervened in the Hasmonean civil war between Hyrcanus II and Aristobulus II, besieged [[jerusalem|Jerusalem]], stormed the [[temple|Temple]] mount, and famously entered the Holy of Holies — finding it empty, to his reported astonishment ([[josephus|Josephus]], *Ant.* 14.4.4; *War* 1.7.6). The event ended Hasmonean independence and inaugurated Roman dominion over Judea. Defeated by Caesar at Pharsalus (48 BCE) and assassinated in Egypt.
