ʿĀṣim ibn Abī al-Najūd al-Asadī (d. 127 AH / 745 CE) — Kufan *qāriʾ* and one of the canonical seven Qurʾānic reciters codified by Ibn Mujāhid in the early 10th century. Inherited his reading from Abū ʿAbd al-Raḥmān al-Sulamī (a Companion-trained reciter) and transmitted it through two main rāwīs: Shuʿba ibn ʿAyyāsh and [[hafs-ibn-sulayman|Ḥafṣ ibn Sulaymān]], the latter of whose transmission ("[[hafs-ibn-sulayman|Ḥafṣ ʿan ʿĀṣim]]") is now the standard recension of the printed [[quran]] across the Sunni world outside the Maghreb.
