Kenite woman, wife of Heber the Kenite, who killed the Canaanite general Sisera by driving a tent-peg through his temple while he slept after the rout of his army by Barak in [[judges|Judges]] 4. Celebrated as "most blessed of women" in the [[song-of-deborah|Song of Deborah]] (Jdg 5:24-27), the earliest substantial Hebrew poem. The narrative is one of the principal female-protagonist deliverance stories of the Hebrew Bible, paralleling [[deborah|Deborah]] and [[judith|Judith]] (Bethulia).
