The unnamed founding figure of the [[qumran|Qumran]] community and key character in the sectarian Dead Sea Scrolls — particularly the Habakkuk Pesher (1QpHab), the Damascus Document (CD), and the *Hodayot* (1QH) thanksgiving hymns. A priest of Zadokite lineage who experienced rejection by a "Wicked Priest" — typically identified by scholarship with one of the Hasmonean high priests Jonathan or Simon (c. 150 BCE) — and led his community into the wilderness in protest at the perceived corruption of the [[temple|Temple]] establishment. His exact historical identity remains unknown.
