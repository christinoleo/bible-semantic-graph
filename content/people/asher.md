Asher, eighth son of [[jacob]], borne by Zilpah — [[leah]]'s handmaid — and reckoned as Leah's (Gen 30:12-13). Name from `ʾāšar`, "happy / blessed": Leah's "Happy am I, for the women will call me happy." Full brother to [[gad]].

Eponymous ancestor of the Tribe of Asher, settled on the Mediterranean coast north of [[mount-carmel]] and west of [[naphtali]], bordering Phoenician territory (Josh 19:24-31). The Jacob blessing (Gen 49:20) is brief and material: "Asher's food shall be rich, and he shall yield royal delicacies." Moses' blessing (Deut 33:24-25) elaborates: "most blessed of sons is Asher ... let him dip his foot in oil," with a famously obscure clause about iron and bronze sandals. In the New Testament, Anna the prophetess who recognizes the infant [[jesus]] in the Temple is identified as "of the tribe of Asher" (Luke 2:36) — a deliberate touch, since the northern tribes had been scattered for centuries by then.
