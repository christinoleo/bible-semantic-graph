Zebulun, tenth son of [[jacob]] and sixth (last) by [[leah]] (Gen 30:19-20); name glossed by Leah from `zābal`, "to honor / dwell with": "now my husband will dwell with me, because I have borne him six sons." Full brother to [[issachar]].

Eponymous ancestor of the Tribe of Zebulun, settled in the lower Galilee between the Mediterranean and the Sea of Galilee, bordering [[issachar]] to the south and [[naphtali]] to the north (Josh 19:10-16). The Jacob blessing (Gen 49:13) gives the maritime image: "Zebulun shall dwell at the haven of the sea; he shall be a haven for ships, and his border shall be at Sidon" — though the tribal allotment as preserved in Joshua doesn't actually reach the coast, a tension critical scholarship reads variously. Moses' blessing (Deut 33:18-19) pairs Zebulun with Issachar at the mountain sacrifices, drawing wealth from "the abundance of the seas and the hidden treasures of the sand." Nazareth itself lies within historic Zebulun territory, making the tribe an implicit setting for the ministry of [[jesus]] (cf. Isa 9:1, Matt 4:13-16).
