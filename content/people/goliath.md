Philistine champion from Gath who defied the armies of Israel in the Valley of Elah and was killed by the young [[david|David]] with a sling-stone and his own sword — narrated in [[1-samuel|1 Samuel]] 17. Described as six cubits and a span tall (~9'9" in MT; ~6'9" in LXX and 4QSam-a). Mentioned in the [[quran|Qurʾān]] (Q2:249-251) as *Jālūt*, defeated by *Dāwūd*, in a confessionally compressed retelling.
