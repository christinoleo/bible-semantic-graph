Bishop of Smyrna (c. 69–c. 155 CE), Apostolic Father, disciple of [[john-the-apostle|John the Apostle]] according to [[irenaeus|Irenaeus]] (who knew Polycarp personally), and martyr. Author of the *Epistle to the Philippians* and subject of the *Martyrdom of Polycarp*, the earliest extant Christian martyr-act outside the New Testament, narrating his death by burning at Smyrna (c. 155 or 167 CE). His refusal "Eighty-six years have I served Him, and He has done me no wrong; how then can I blaspheme my King who has saved me?" is among the most-quoted lines of early Christian witness.
