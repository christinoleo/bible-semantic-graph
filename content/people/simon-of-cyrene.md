A man from Cyrene in North Africa, father of Alexander and Rufus ([[gospel-of-mark|Mk]] 15:21), compelled by the Roman cohort to carry the *patibulum* (crossbeam) of [[jesus|Jesus]] on the way to Golgotha (Mt 27:32; Mk 15:21; Lk 23:26). Mark's mention of his sons by name presupposes their identifiability in the early Roman Christian community. The second-century Gnostic Basilides argued — against the canonical account — that Simon was crucified in Jesus's place, a docetic substitutionist reading; the same view is preserved in the [[quran|Qurʾān]] (Q4:157, *shubbiha la-hum*) and is the textual basis of the Islamic [[jesus-crucifixion-islamic-reading|denial of the crucifixion]].
