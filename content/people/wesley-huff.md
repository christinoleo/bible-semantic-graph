Wesley Livingstone Huff (b. 1991) — Canadian Christian apologist and Reformed Baptist; Vice President of Apologetics Canada; PhD candidate in New Testament at [[wycliffe-college]], University of Toronto, with a dissertation treating ancient New Testament [[manuscripts-as-historical-artifacts]] — i.e., as material objects whose paleography, codicology, and provenance encode social indicators about the communities that produced them, rather than as transparent vehicles for a text. BA Sociology (York University), MTS (Tyndale University). Elder at West Toronto Baptist Church.

Born in Multan, Pakistan — a biographical coincidence with [[nabeel-qureshi]], with whom he shares no theological lineage. At age 11 a rare neurological condition left him temporarily paralyzed from the waist down; the recovery, medically unexplained, is part of the conversion-narrative register he uses publicly.

The viral arc came through the [[youtube-channel-weshuff|@WesHuff YouTube channel]] (780k+ subscribers) and high-platform podcast appearances — Joe Rogan Experience (January 2025, defending the historicity of the resurrection), Diary of a CEO, Piers Morgan Uncensored, Flagrant with Andrew Schulz, Shawn Ryan Show, Peterson Academy. His "Can I Trust the Bible" series is his signature popularizing register. Distinctive in the [[christian-apologetic]] field for sustained focus on **manuscript-and-canon questions** — the specific topics where popular skepticism (Constantine invented the Bible, Nicaea voted on the canon, lost gospels were suppressed) most often outruns the historical record.
