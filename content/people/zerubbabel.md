Davidic-line governor of the Persian province of Yehud (c. 520–510 BCE), grandson of King Jehoiachin, who led the second wave of returnees from Babylonian exile and oversaw the rebuilding of the [[temple|Second Temple]] completed in 516 BCE ([[ezra|Ezra]] 3-6; [[book-of-haggai|Haggai]] 1-2; [[book-of-zechariah|Zechariah]] 4). The prophet Haggai addresses him as "my servant" with messianic overtones (Hag 2:23); his name appears in the Davidic genealogies of [[gospel-of-matthew|Matt]] 1:12-13 and [[gospel-of-luke|Lk]] 3:27. The "signet ring" passage cancels the curse on Jehoiachin (Jer 22:24).
