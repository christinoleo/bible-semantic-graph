A *ḥanīf* (pre-Islamic monotheist) and Christian of Mecca, cousin of [[khadija]], to whom she brought [[muhammad|Muhammad]] after the first [[muhammad-prophetic-call|Ḥirāʾ encounter]]. Waraqa identified the experience as the *Nāmūs* (sometimes glossed as [[gabriel|Gabriel]]) "that had come to [[moses|Moses]]," and reportedly affirmed that if he lived long enough he would defend Muhammad's prophethood. He died shortly afterward. Christian scholars have argued his form of Christianity was likely Ebionite or otherwise heterodox — an observation that bears on the absence of trinitarian theology in his confirmation.
