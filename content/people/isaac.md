Isaac is the son of [[abraham]] and [[sarah]], the second of the patriarchs. He is the principal human figure of the [[sacrifice-of-isaac]] narrative — though, notably, he speaks only once in it. Later tradition (rabbinic and Christian) has read his near-sacrifice as foundational, despite the Genesis text's narrative reticence.
