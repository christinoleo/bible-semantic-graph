American evangelical writer (b. 1929), author of *The Late Great Planet Earth* (1970) — at one point the bestselling non-fiction book of the 1970s — which presented [[dispensationalism|dispensationalist]] eschatology as imminent prophecy fulfilled in the post-1948 Israeli state. Like [[tim-lahaye|LaHaye]], Lindsey locates the [[antichrist]] in a European-led revived Roman Empire, contrasting with [[joel-richardson|Joel Richardson]]'s later Islamic-Antichrist reading.
