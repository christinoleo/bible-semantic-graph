Singer, Tovia (born 1960) — American Orthodox rabbi; founder and director of Outreach Judaism, the counter-missionary organization established in 1994 to respond to evangelical Christian missionary activity directed at Jews. Trained in Orthodox yeshivot; broadcaster on Israel National Radio; the most publicly visible figure of the contemporary [[jewish-anti-missionary]] movement and a frequent debate opponent of Christian apologists, [[michael-brown]] foremost among them.

The argumentative program is a sustained refutation of the messianic-prophecy proof-texts that Christian missionaries deploy from the Hebrew Bible — [[isaiah-53]] read as Israel-the-suffering-servant rather than as a prediction of [[jesus]]; [[psalm-22]] read against its New Testament crucifixion-reading; the seventy weeks of [[daniel-9]] read against the Christian messianic-dating tradition; the virgin of Isaiah 7:14 read as *ʿalmah* (young woman) rather than *betulah* (virgin) and as an eighth-century-BCE sign to Ahaz rather than a Christmas prophecy. *Let's Get Biblical!* (1998, expanded 2014) is the print summa of the program; the long-running audio series and radio show carry it to a wider lay audience. Responds_to [[christian-apologetic]] on its own evidentiary terms — Tanakh in the Masoretic Hebrew, contextual reading, refusal of christological pre-interpretation — and is therefore read across the [[jewish-anti-missionary]] world as the definitive popular voice.
