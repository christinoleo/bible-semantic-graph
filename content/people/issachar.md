Issachar, ninth son of [[jacob]] and fifth by [[leah]] (Gen 30:14-18); conceived after the mandrakes episode in which Leah trades Rachel a night with Jacob for the love-apples her son [[reuben]] had found. Name glossed as `yēš śākār`, "there is a reward / hire" — Leah's "God has given me my hire because I gave my maid to my husband."

Eponymous ancestor of the Tribe of Issachar, which settled in the fertile Jezreel valley between [[zebulun]] to the north and Manasseh to the south. The Jacob blessing (Gen 49:14-15) is barbed: "Issachar is a strong-boned ass, lying down between the sheepfolds; he saw that a resting-place was good ... so he bowed his shoulder to bear, and became a slave at forced labor" — the agricultural prosperity of the valley read as the price of submission. Moses' blessing (Deut 33:18-19) pairs Issachar with Zebulun in rejoicing at their tents and offering right sacrifices on the mountain. 1 Chr 12:32 famously names "the sons of Issachar" as those "who had understanding of the times, to know what Israel ought to do."
