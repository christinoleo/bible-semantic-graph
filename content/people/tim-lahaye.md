American evangelical pastor and author (1926–2016), co-author of the *Left Behind* novel series (1995–2007, with Jerry B. Jenkins) which popularized [[dispensationalism|dispensationalist]] pretribulational [[premillennialism]] for a mass audience. LaHaye located the [[antichrist]] in a revived Roman / European empire — a Western reading that [[joel-richardson|Joel Richardson]]'s Islamic-Antichrist thesis explicitly inverts.
