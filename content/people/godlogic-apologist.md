Avery — African-American Christian street and livestream apologist; host of the YouTube channel *GodLogic Apologetics*. Works opposite Muslim, Jewish, and atheist interlocutors in both the YouTube long-form debate register (with [[muhammad-hijab]], [[hamza-of-hamzas-den]]) and on-the-ground street-mic dialogues around Muslim communities (Dearborn, Speakers' Corner). Argumentative repertoire centres on [[isaiah-53-christian-reading|Isaiah 53 read messianically]], the [[claim-muhammad-confirms-jesus-so-cannot-contradict-him|Quranic confirmation argument against Islam]], the [[claim-quran-commands-following-extant-injil|Q 5:47 instruction-to-judge-by-the-Gospel]] case, [[claim-q19-93-closes-honorific-son-reading|Q 19:93 against the honorific-son escape]], and a [[claim-hafs-trust-inconsistency-undercuts-gospel-author-rejection|tu quoque on Ḥafṣ ʿan ʿĀṣim]] when Muslims press Gospel-author reliability. The channel also features the female commentator known as Lady Logic on shared-format content.
