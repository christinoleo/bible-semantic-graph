Fourth son of [[david|David]] and Haggith (2 Sam 3:4), eldest surviving son after the deaths of Amnon, Chileab, and Absalom, who attempted to claim the throne in David's last days by being proclaimed king at En-Rogel with Joab and the priest Abiathar. The counter-coup by Nathan, Bathsheba, Zadok, and Benaiah secured [[solomon|Solomon]]'s succession (1 Kgs 1). After David's death, Adonijah's request for David's last attendant Abishag the Shunammite was interpreted by Solomon as a renewed claim to the throne, and Adonijah was executed by Benaiah (1 Kgs 2:13-25).
