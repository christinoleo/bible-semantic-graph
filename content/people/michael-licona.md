Michael Licona (b. 1961) — American New Testament historian (Houston Christian University) and co-architect with [[gary-habermas]] of the [[minimal-facts-approach]] to the [[resurrection-of-jesus]]. His 2010 monograph *The Resurrection of Jesus: A New Historiographical Approach* extends the framework into formal historiography (inference to best explanation, criteria for assessing miracle claims).

Licona is the scholar [[nabeel-qureshi]] cites alongside Habermas as the source of the resurrection argument in *[[no-god-but-one]]*.
