Naphtali, sixth son of [[jacob]], borne by Bilhah — [[rachel]]'s handmaid — and reckoned as Rachel's (Gen 30:7-8). Name from `niptal`, "to wrestle": Rachel's claim that "with mighty wrestlings I have wrestled with my sister and prevailed." Full brother to [[dan]].

Eponymous ancestor of the Tribe of Naphtali, which settled in the Galilee region north and west of the Sea of Galilee, bordering [[asher]] to the west and [[zebulun]] to the south. The Jacob blessing (Gen 49:21) gives the elliptical image: "Naphtali is a hind let loose, that bears comely fawns" — read variously as praise of swiftness, freedom, or eloquence. Moses' blessing (Deut 33:23) calls Naphtali "satisfied with favor, full of the blessing of YHWH." The territory falls early under Assyrian deportation (2 Kgs 15:29) and is later named in Isa 9:1-2 / Matt 4:13-16 as "Galilee of the nations" where the great light dawns — geography that becomes Christologically loaded.
