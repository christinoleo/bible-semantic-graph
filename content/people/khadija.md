First wife of [[muhammad|Muhammad]] (married c. 595 CE), a Meccan widow and successful caravan merchant fifteen years his senior who first employed him as a commercial agent before proposing marriage. After the [[muhammad-prophetic-call|Ḥirāʾ encounter]] she reassured the frightened Muhammad that the experience was not demonic, then took him to her Christian cousin [[waraqa-ibn-nawfal|Waraqa ibn Nawfal]] who identified the visitor as the *Nāmūs* that had come to Moses. Islamic tradition holds her as the first convert to Islam.
