American Christian author known for advancing the *Islamic Antichrist* thesis — the position that the biblical [[antichrist]] is to be identified with the [[mahdi|Islamic Mahdī]], and the Beast of [[revelation|Revelation]] 13 with a global Islamic caliphate rather than with a revived Roman or European empire. Principal works: *The Islamic Antichrist* (2009), *Mideast Beast* (2012), *Mystery Babylon* (2017).
