Dutch Reformed theologian (1854–1921), with [[abraham-kuyper|Abraham Kuyper]] one of the two principal architects of the Neo-Calvinist movement at the turn of the twentieth century. Professor at the Theological School at Kampen and later at the Free University of Amsterdam. Author of *Reformed Dogmatics* (1895-1901), still the standard systematic theology of the Dutch Reformed tradition — encyclopedic, philosophically engaged, and influential in modern Anglophone Reformed thought through John Bolt's translation.
