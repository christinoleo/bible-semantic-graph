Persian Islamic scholar (810–870 CE), born in Bukhara, compiler of *[[sahih-al-bukhari|Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī]]* — the most authoritative Sunni hadith collection, traditionally said to be the result of sifting some 600,000 reports down to roughly 7,275 sound traditions over sixteen years of travel and isnād-criticism. Also authored *al-Tārīkh al-Kabīr*, a foundational biographical dictionary of hadith transmitters. The work of al-Bukhārī (with that of his contemporary [[muslim-ibn-hajjaj|Muslim ibn al-Ḥajjāj]]) defines the canonical Sunni hadith tradition.
