Dan, fifth son of [[jacob]], borne by Bilhah — [[rachel]]'s handmaid — and reckoned as Rachel's (Gen 30:5-6). Name from `dîn`, "to judge": Rachel's claim that "God has judged me" and given her a son through her servant. The Jacob blessing (Gen 49:16-18) plays on it again: "Dan shall judge his people ... a serpent in the way."

Eponymous ancestor of the Tribe of Dan. The tribe's original allotment lay on the coast (Josh 19:40-48), but pressed by the Amorites and Philistines it migrated north and seized the city of Laish in the upper Galilee, renaming it Dan (Judg 18) — an extended narrative of tribal displacement, idolatry (the Danites carry off Micah's household idols and Levite), and the founding of a rival sanctuary. The phrase "from Dan to Beersheba" comes to name the full north-south span of the land. The Tribe of Dan is conspicuously absent from the sealed tribes of Rev 7:5-8, a gap on which patristic and rabbinic speculation built much.
