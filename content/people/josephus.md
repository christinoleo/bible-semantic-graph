First-century Jewish historian (c. 37–100 CE), born Yosef ben Matityahu in a priestly family in Jerusalem. Participated in the First Jewish-Roman War as commander in Galilee before defecting to the Romans under Vespasian and Titus; authored *The Jewish War*, *Antiquities of the Jews*, *Against Apion*, and his *Life*. His *Antiquities* 18.63-64 contains the *Testimonium Flavianum*, the principal non-Christian source attesting the historical [[jesus]] and the [[crucifixion-of-jesus|crucifixion under Pilate]]; *Antiquities* 20.200 attests [[james-brother-of-jesus|James the brother of Jesus]].
