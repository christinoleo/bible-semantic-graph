Abraham (originally Abram) is the first patriarch in the Hebrew Bible. Called by [[yhwh]] to leave [[ur]] for [[canaan]], he becomes the recipient of the [[abrahamic-covenant]] — the promise of land, descendants, and divine blessing.

The narrative arc culminates in the [[sacrifice-of-isaac]] at [[moriah]], a passage that has been read variously as a test of faith, a polemic against child sacrifice, and a prefiguration of later atonement theology.
