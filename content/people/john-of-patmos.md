The first-century Christian author of the [[revelation|Book of Revelation]], traditionally identified with [[john-the-apostle|John the Apostle]] (son of Zebedee) writing in exile on the Aegean island of Patmos under Domitian (c. 95 CE), though some modern scholars distinguish him as a separate figure ("John the Elder"). Whatever his exact identity, his apocalyptic vision is the principal source of the figure of the [[antichrist|Beast]] and of the [[second-coming-of-christ|returning Christ on the white horse]] in [[white-horse-rider-of-revelation|Rev 19:11-16]].
