Held by [[twelver-shia|Twelver Shīʿīs]] to be Muḥammad ibn al-Ḥasan al-ʿAskarī, born c. 869 CE in Sāmarrāʾ, the twelfth and final imam in succession from [[ali-ibn-abi-talib|ʿAlī ibn Abī Ṭālib]]. Shīʿī tradition holds he entered the Lesser Occultation in 874 CE and the Greater Occultation in 941 CE, remaining alive but hidden until his eschatological return as [[mahdi|al-Mahdī al-Muntaẓar]] (the Awaited Mahdī) to establish global justice before the descent of [[returning-isa-islamic-eschatology|ʿĪsā]]. Sunni eschatology accepts a Mahdī figure but rejects the identification with this specific historical person.
