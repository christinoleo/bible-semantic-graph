Mirza Ghulam Ahmad (1835–1908) — Punjabi religious teacher who in 1889 founded the [[ahmadiyya]] movement at Qadian, claiming to be the [[muhammad-al-mahdi|promised Mahdi]], the returned Messiah ([[jesus]] having not bodily ascended but died naturally in Kashmir — see the [[claim-ahmadiyya-survival-thesis|survival thesis]]), and a non-law-bearing prophet in continuity with [[muhammad]]. The combination of these claims puts the movement in formal collision with the mainstream Sunni reading of [[khatam-an-nabiyyin|Q 33:40]] (Muhammad as Seal of the Prophets), and Ahmadis have been declared non-Muslim by Pakistani law (1974) and by Al-Azhar.

His 1908 *Masih Hindustan Mein* (*Jesus in India*) is the founding text of the Kashmir-tomb tradition that [[nabeel-qureshi]] inherited as a child and later argued against in *[[seeking-allah-finding-jesus]]*.
