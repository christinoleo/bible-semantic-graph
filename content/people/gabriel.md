The angel identified across Jewish, Christian, and Islamic tradition as the principal messenger of divine revelation. In the Hebrew Bible Gabriel explains visions to [[daniel-the-prophet|Daniel]] ([[book-of-daniel|Dan]] 8:16, 9:21). In the New Testament he announces the births of John the Baptist (Lk 1:11-20) and [[jesus]] (Lk 1:26-38) to Zechariah and [[mary-mother-of-jesus|Mary]]. In Islam, Jibrīl is the named conduit of the [[quran|Qurʾān]] to [[muhammad|Muhammad]] across all twenty-three years of revelation, beginning at [[muhammad-prophetic-call|Ḥirāʾ]] — though Muhammad's own initial reading of the encounter was [[claim-muhammad-feared-demonic-possession-at-hira|fear of demonic possession]] rather than recognition of Gabriel.
