The angelic prince described in [[book-of-daniel|Daniel]] 10:13, 10:21, 12:1 as "the great prince who has charge of your people" (Israel), and in [[revelation|Revelation]] 12:7-9 as the angelic warrior who defeats the dragon. [[jude|Jude]] 9 references his dispute with the devil over the body of [[moses]]. In Islamic tradition (Q2:98) [[gabriel|Jibrīl]] and Mīkhāʾīl are paired as the chief angels. Patron-angel theology develops the figure further in medieval Catholic and Eastern Orthodox piety.
