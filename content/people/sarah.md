Sarah, wife of [[abraham]] and mother of [[isaac]], is the first of the matriarchs. Her name change from Sarai to Sarah accompanies the covenantal renaming of [[abraham]] (Gen 17), structurally signalling her co-participation in the [[abrahamic-covenant]].
